# Ink Gesture Poster Zine Prompt Recipes

Use this file when selecting gesture family, accent, or text policy.

## Recipe Matrix

### central-swirl

Use for: concentration, breath, self, motion, wind, spirit, or a single abstract statement.

Prompt ingredients:

- one vertical or diagonal swirl
- dark core with feathered outer strokes
- ample white field
- one tiny accent drop or orb

### orbit-loop

Use for: orbit, echo, memory, pull, energy, or suspended motion.

Prompt ingredients:

- looping arcs around a central knot
- thin trailing filaments
- optional faint circular stain
- small accent mark near the motion center

### vertical-thread

Use for: falling, ascent, descent, rain, smoke, drift, or fragile continuity.

Prompt ingredients:

- long vertical gesture
- tapering lower tail
- sparse drips and flecks
- one restrained accent near midline or lower third

### split-stroke

Use for: contrast, duality, pause, before/after, or relation.

Prompt ingredients:

- two gestures separated by paper space
- one darker and one lighter
- minimal microtext only if needed

### suspended-splash

Use for: release, impact, bloom, sudden feeling, or energetic abstracted movement.

Prompt ingredients:

- floating ink bloom
- radial tendrils
- one strong accent circle, dot, or splash
- paper left open around it

### brush-figure

Use for: body-memory, spirit, trace, gesture-as-person, or abstract portrait feeling.

Prompt ingredients:

- figure-like brush movement without literal anatomy
- soft torso core and trailing limbs of ink
- quiet negative space

## Theme Translation

For a name or phrase:

- render it as mood only unless the user explicitly asks for text.

For water, breath, smoke, or wind:

- use swirls, drips, and feathered edges.

For body, spirit, or portrait:

- use a figure-like brush motion rather than a literal person.

For energy or tension:

- add a small accent orb or a sharper black stroke.

## Prompt Skeleton

```text
Portrait 3:4 raster image on a tall white or cool-white paper field, huge negative space, flat scanned-paper light, matte fibers, no border, no mockup.

One dominant abstract ink gesture in the chosen family, placed [center / upper-middle / slightly off-center], with clear motion path, trailing filaments, soft absorption, feathered edges, and a calm editorial balance.

Texture: ink bleed, dry-brush fracture, watery pooling, slight speckle, and paper tooth; keep the gesture crisp enough to read at thumbnail size while the surrounding field stays quiet.

Typography: [textless / tiny phrase / tiny note only], with [accent color and form] as a small but visible counterpoint; no long text, no title block unless requested.

Mood: contemplative, minimal, contemporary, poetic; avoid landscapes, scenes, UI, logos, collage clutter, glossy render, neon, and commercial poster hierarchy.
```
