# Style Grammar

Use this file when analyzing a reference, correcting drift, or tightening the prompt.

## Visual DNA

- The page reads like a ticket card floating in a black void.
- The card is the subject: a die-cut white panel with punched scallops, not a UI component.
- The composition is graphic, theatrical, and high contrast.
- The illustration behaves like a printed film still, stage poster, or old program engraving.

## Composition

- Canvas: vertical 3:4, 4:5, or 3:5.
- Background: mostly pure black or near-black empty space.
- Main card: one dominant white ticket occupying about 55%-80% of the canvas height.
- Repeat strip: optional narrow strip on the right, usually 15%-25% of the width.
- Edge rhythm: top and bottom scallops or half-moon punches, often mirrored.
- Structure: title at top, illustration in upper half, metadata block below, optional slogan at the bottom.

## Illustration Language

Use one central visual metaphor, not a full scene dump:

- spotlighted figure on stage
- surreal room or corridor
- animal or creature silhouette
- symbolic object machine
- moon, star, curtain, house, lamp, or frame-within-frame

The drawing should feel hand-printed, not glossy or photorealistic.

## Typography

- Main title: heavy slab serif, condensed serif, distressed display, or blunt theatrical grotesk.
- Subtitle: smaller English line or genre line.
- Chinese title: short, bold, and integrated into the card layout.
- Metadata: rows like hall, seat, price, date, time, runtime, or credits.
- Avoid long paragraphs. Keep the lower block ticket-like and scannable.

## Color

- Default: black and white.
- Optional spot accent: red, yellow, pink, or cream.
- The accent can fill a burst, stamp, title bar, or panel, but should not become a multicolor palette.
- If the output is too plain, add one accent only. If it is too busy, return to monochrome.

## Texture

Use concrete print language:

- photocopy grain
- halftone speckle
- ink drag
- worn paper fibers
- stamp bleed
- rough screenprint edges
- chalky black fill
- slight misregistration

## Drift Corrections

- Too modern: add `old film-program poster`, `distressed slab serif`, `ticket stub layout`, `no app UI`.
- Too decorative: add `strict ticket-card grid`, `black void surround`, `no scrapbook`.
- Too colorful: add `black and white base`, `one spot color only`.
- Too clean: add `photocopy grain`, `halftone speckle`, `worn print edges`.
- Too empty: add `metadata rows`, `repeat strip`, `slogan line`.
- Too crowded: keep one illustration panel and one metadata block.
