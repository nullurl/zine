# Prompt Recipes

## Five-Paragraph Base Prompt

```text
Tall vertical 3:5 literary archive poster on warm aged ivory paper, 65%-85% quiet paper field, subtle fibers, stains, xerox softness, and matte scanned texture. No border, device mockup, card shadow, or clean digital white.

Upper-left editorial scaffold: small [accent] index square with [number], tiny uppercase [section label], large [author] in high-contrast dark serif, and oversized italic outline [title/keyword] slightly overlapping below. Upper-right tiny [edition/location/date] metadata. Keep generous left alignment and quiet right-side paper.

Place the exact short quote in [number] lines of literary italic serif in the upper-left/middle column: “[verbatim quote]”. Use one oversized quotation mark and emphasize only “[verbatim key clause]” in [accent]. Add a thin rule and tiny attribution “[verbatim attribution]”.

Lower-right/lower-center: one restrained [photograph/material anchor] emerging from torn paper, low-contrast grayscale or muted monochrome, with [one supporting abstract mark]. It occupies 15%-30% of the canvas and remains subordinate to the typography. Use halftone, paper tear, photocopy softness, ink bleed, and one repeated accent detail.

Footer: tiny [archive label], small page/index mark, thin rule or corner bracket. Avoid social quote card, corporate presentation, generic book cover, long text, invented attribution, copied reference text, logo, watermark, glossy CGI, HDR, neon, purple grading, teal-orange grading, and scrapbook clutter.
```

## Arrival and Old Architecture

Use for death, arrival, return, memory, or ancestral place.

- accent: rust red or burnt orange
- lower anchor: faded courtyard gate, stone path, old wall, autumn branches, or doorway
- supporting marks: torn monochrome photo, red leaves, thin vertical metadata
- quote: 3-5 lines, final clause in rust
- outline keyword: `ARRIVE`, `RETURN`, or an exact user word

Keep the architecture low contrast and allow paper to surround it.

## Bird and Breakthrough

Use for birth, freedom, rupture, change, and philosophical awakening.

- accent: cobalt blue
- lower anchor: egg, bird silhouette, orbit diagram, blue torn paper, or geometric field
- supporting marks: dotted arcs, small black/blue points, one broken shell or paper seam
- outline keyword: `WORLD`, `BORN`, or an exact user word

Use a single bird or wing gesture, not a flock illustration.

## Desert and Survival

Use for heat, endurance, exile, travel, and memory.

- accent: burnt orange
- lower anchor: grainy desert photograph, fabric, sun disc, or old travel image
- supporting marks: torn sand-colored paper and one circular stamp-like mark
- outline keyword: `DESERT`, `SUMMER`, or an exact user word

## Room and Interior Life

Use for solitude, writing, private space, and thought.

- accent: muted violet
- lower anchor: desk, chair, window, room drawing, empty interior, or translucent curtain
- supporting marks: line-drawn branch, purple window block, faint floor reflection
- outline keyword: `ROOM`, `ALONE`, or an exact user word

## Field and Aloneness

Use for quiet landscape, loneliness, growth, and distance.

- accent: leaf green
- lower anchor: gray mountain or field print with one tiny person or animal
- supporting marks: grass linework and pale torn paper
- outline keyword: `ALONE`, `FIELD`, or an exact user word

## Regeneration Recipes

### Typography collapsed

Add: `fixed editorial hierarchy: author largest at top-left, outline keyword second, quote block third, one highlighted final clause, microtype last; no centered layout`.

### Image too large

Add: `lower collage occupies only 20% of the page, with at least 55% uninterrupted cream paper remaining around text and image`.

### Accent too weak

Add: `fully saturated opaque [accent] in the index square and highlighted final clause, with one matching material detail, visible at thumbnail size`.

### Too commercial

Add: `archival literary study, imperfect paper, low-contrast photocopy fragment, no branding, no polished advertising, no glossy art direction`.

### Text hallucination

Use a 2-6 word title and a 1-3 line quote, state each line verbatim, or reserve blank text areas for post-generation typesetting.
