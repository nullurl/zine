# Earth Ink Poem Poster Structure

## Reverse-engineered source DNA

- Warm aged paper or parchment-like field.
- One dominant calligraphy or brush-typed phrase.
- A thin underline, rule, or separator often supports the main phrase.
- Secondary microtext sits lower, to the side, or in a narrow column.
- An inset window, dark block, or label may hold the title or a small phrase.
- Colors stay earthy: black, gray, sepia, burnt orange, ochre, dark brown.
- The paper carries folds, stains, creases, or scan grain.
- Mood is literary, severe, antique, and quietly experimental.

## Layout families

- **hero-calligraphy:** a single oversized text mass dominates the page.
- **framed-inset:** a black, cream, or ochre inset carries a phrase or texture window.
- **left-ink-right-column:** bold text mass left or center-left, thin text column right.
- **split-board:** multiple editorial zones like a poster board or exhibition sheet.
- **stained-paper-center:** text and accent gather in the middle of a large paper field.
- **archival-board:** poster-like arrangement with tape, tabs, labels, and small notes.

## Text hierarchy

- Main phrase: large, hand-brushed, rough printed, or letterpress-like.
- Secondary phrase: smaller, evenly tracked, or stacked under a rule.
- Microtext: tiny paragraph, caption, quote source, or poetic note.
- Accent label: one colored block, tape piece, or inset tag.
- Avoid making all text the same weight.

## Texture rules

- Old paper fibers, folds, scan noise, dust, faint grid, rough edges.
- Black or sepia ink should feel absorbed into paper.
- Burnt orange or ochre accents should look like labels, tape, or printed blocks.
- Dark panels or framed windows may be used, but keep them worn and matte.

## Prompt recipe

1. Canvas and paper field: ratio, tone, empty space, wear.
2. Primary text mass: phrase, placement, scale, handwriting or print style.
3. Support structure: underline, column, label, inset, or frame.
4. Texture and defects: folds, stains, dust, bleed, grid, roughness.
5. Mood and avoids: antique literary tone plus hard constraints.

## Hard avoids

- Clean corporate flyer
- Minimal poster with no texture
- Full-bleed photo scene
- Bright multi-color palette
- UI layout or web card design
- Dense scrapbook clutter
- Modern sans-serif-only treatment
- Neon, glossy, or polished advertising style

## Example prompt skeleton

```text
Tall aged-paper poster with wide empty margins, visible folds, dust, and scan grain.

Place one oversized calligraphic Chinese phrase near the left-center, with a thin underline and a smaller supporting line below it.

Add a narrow microtext column on the right and one small burnt-orange label block or inset panel; keep the ink earthy and worn.

Make it literary, archival, and quiet; avoid clean flyer hierarchy, bright colors, glossy mockup, UI styling, and full-bleed imagery.
```
