# Textile Swatch Zine Prompt Recipes v0.3

Use this file when a request needs a concrete layout, a batch variation, or help translating an abstract theme into materials.

## Recipe Matrix

### balanced-six

Use for: default textile-board requests, reference fusion, balanced editorial sample pages.

Prompt ingredients:

- 2x3 frosted sample-card grid in the upper half
- four material cards, one title/index card, one small accent or notation card
- large blank lower field
- tiny black, gray-green, or soft type with one accent swatch

Good themes: "spring", "cafe", "island", "rain", "quiet studio", "linen morning".

### material-ledger

Use for: archival, bespoke, workshop, library, studio, specification-like mood.

Prompt ingredients:

- left-side stacked material samples
- right-side tiny index labels, dots, ticks, and codes
- small ruled line or sample number
- one clear title card, no brand or SKU

Good themes: "tailor archive", "paper mill", "coffee shop materials", "stone and wool".

### single-hero-swatch

Use for: one dominant material or product-neutral hero study.

Prompt ingredients:

- one large tactile swatch, slightly above center
- four small frosted label or texture cards around it
- title beneath or on a small card
- very wide paper margins

Good themes: "black wool", "blue satin", "rain fabric", "old notebook cover".

### thread-route

Use for: yarn, knitting, sewing, route, travel, memory, diary-like themes.

Prompt ingredients:

- five or six sample cards
- one loose thread travels between cards
- a knot, stitch, or loop marks the accent
- tiny labels follow the thread path

Good themes: "my rainy day", "island path", "spring thread", "coffee steam".

### dye-study

Use for: color, weather, botanical dye, seasonal gradient, ink or paper studies.

Prompt ingredients:

- repeated same fabric or handmade paper sample
- three to five tonal variants
- one stronger dye chip as accent
- tiny lab-style title and date-like code

Good themes: "tropical green", "rain blue", "cafe brown with cobalt note", "little spring".

### construction-grid

Use for: textile structure, craft, fabrication, technical material comparisons.

Prompt ingredients:

- precise 3x2 grid
- different weave or knit structures in the same palette
- tiny construction labels such as WEFT, LOOP, RIB, PILE
- strict scan view and soft shadows

Good themes: "woven island", "winter cloth", "architectural fabric", "quiet uniforms".

### graphic-label-board

Use for: reference images that show soft labels, tabs, film strips, ticket shapes, dots, arrows, or blank stationery elements.

Prompt ingredients:

- bright pearl-white or sea-glass mint paper
- 10-24 small pieces arranged as a calm sticker-sheet or material notation board
- rounded rectangles, pill strips, ticket tabs, film perforations, punched circles, bracket corners, dotted rails, and one or two textile/paper chips
- high-key scanned look with shallow shadows and no dark background
- mostly textless, with only tiny dots, ticks, lines, counters, or one short index word

Good themes: "soft archive labels", "quiet film notes", "paper materials", "mint studio kit", "cloud itinerary".

### color-preserved-reference

Use for: a supplied image whose palette should remain recognizable.

Prompt ingredients:

- one or two larger cards carry the reference's dominant hues
- smaller chips echo those hues in paler paper, thread, or vellum
- keep the reference palette continuous rather than reducing it to beige
- preserve graphic shape language when the reference is label-like

Good themes: "preserve this color", "based on this image", "use the reference palette".

Use one recipe only. Do not combine every sample type, label board, thread route, dye chart, and hero panel in one image.

## Theme Translation

For "spring":

- pearl white paper, pale green cotton, ivory loop yarn, butter-yellow stitch, soft label tabs, tiny title "A LITTLE SPRING"

For "rain":

- cool gray paper, translucent blue dyed cotton, charcoal wool, silver-gray satin, pale thread route, tiny title "RAIN SAMPLE"

For "island" or "tropical":

- bright off-white paper, moss green linen, banana-leaf green silk, sand-colored raw cotton, turquoise thread, title "ISLAND MATERIALS"

For "cafe":

- warm white paper, espresso wool, cream boucle, steel-blue satin or paper chip, receipt-like tiny index, title "CAFE SWATCHES"

For "night":

- keep paper bright enough; use charcoal wool as one sample, cobalt stitch as accent, pale gray vellum-like card shadows, title "NIGHT CLOTH"

For "soft label reference":

- bright pearl paper, sea-glass mint panels, fog-gray vellum, rounded rectangle cards, film-strip holes, punched circles, thin rules, dotted progress rails, mostly textless

## Prompt Skeleton

Use this structure and replace bracketed fields:

```text
Portrait [2:3 or 3:4] flat-lay scan on [paper tone] fibrous paper, [negative-space share] visible blank paper, soft diffuse daylight, matte surface, high-key low-contrast exposure, no border or mockup.

[Layout family] arranged [placement], [card or chip count] frosted translucent paper sample slips and label chips with slight rounded corners, shallow contact shadows, [blank-space placement].

Material and label system includes [sample 1], [sample 2], [sample 3], [sample 4], plus [graphic forms: punched holes, ticket tabs, film-strip perforations, dotted rails, bracket corners, pill strips, circles, arrows] and [accent material/color] as the only chromatic anchor.

Tiny archival typography: title "[SHORT TITLE]" or mostly textless, [subtitle or index treatment], modern sans/typewriter/sample-label type, no long copy, no brand names.

Quiet bespoke material archive mood, paper fibers, scan noise, small print imperfections, shallow physical depth; avoid UI cards, retail catalogs, product branding, glossy 3D, heavy shadows, dense scrapbook decoration, copied text, watermarks, and long paragraphs.
```

## Title Bank

Use original short titles. Examples:

- MATERIAL NOTE
- THREAD WEATHER
- A LITTLE SPRING
- RAIN SAMPLE
- ISLAND MATERIALS
- CAFE SWATCHES
- GREEN STUDY
- SILK AND PAPER
- SOFT CONSTRUCTION
- FIELD CLOTH
- LABEL STUDY
- QUIET INDEX
- PAPER WEATHER

Keep title text short because image models distort long labels. For shape-led references, textless is often stronger.
