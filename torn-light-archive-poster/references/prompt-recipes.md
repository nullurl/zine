# Prompt Recipes

Use these templates when a request needs prompt-only output, batch variation, or a subject that does not immediately map to the core style.

## Reverse-Engineered Base Prompt

```text
Tall vertical flat orthographic scan of a handmade archival paper collage on deckled blue-gray fibrous paper. The paper fills the whole frame, with worn edges, embedded dust, pulp texture, low contrast, and no mockup shadow.

Create one dominant irregular torn photographic window about [SUBJECT], occupying about [SIZE]% of the poster, with exposed white paper fibers along the tear. The photo should feel like [TIME/SEASON], with deep blue-gray shadows and small warm light points. Add [2-5] smaller torn scraps around it: [SUPPORT FRAGMENTS].

Layer translucent vellum, rice paper, or tissue strips partly over the photo edges. Add [BOTANICAL TRACE] as a delicate printed or pressed-paper element. Add thin black [LINEWORK SYSTEM] across the margins and paper field: measurement ticks, crosshairs, dotted paths, contour lines, or architectural sketch marks.

Set tiny typewriter microtext on the paper: "[MAIN LABEL]" and "[SUBTITLE]". The text is small, ink-worn, aligned like an archive note, not a headline.

Restrained gray and blue palette, matte paper, deep blue photographic fragments, small warm amber or brass light accents, scan noise, halftone wear, torn fiber detail. Avoid glossy scrapbook, 3D paper layers, drop shadows, clean UI white, commercial poster, logo, CTA, cute journaling, anime, neon, cinematic full-bleed scene.
```

## Subject Mappings

- **city:** blue-hour towers, wet street, glass facades, traffic lights, architectural elevation marks, label `CITY / AFTERGLOW`.
- **snow:** dark tree line, street lamps, blue snow, falling specks, lamp-post blueprint, label `SNOW / LAMP`.
- **river / bloom:** water glare, blossom branch, translucent water-shaped tissue, botanical specimen, label `BLOOM / CURRENT`.
- **window / home:** warm wooden window, lantern, hanging branches, roofline sketch, label `BLUE HOUR / WARM WINDOW`.
- **fireworks / memory:** deep blue sky scraps, phone silhouette, gold dotted trajectories, starburst linework, label `LIGHT / HELD`.
- **winter / glow:** tree lights, pine needles, ornament red, snowflake diagram, label `WINTER / GLOW`.

## Batch Variation Rules

For a batch, vary at least three axes per image:

- layout: central window, diagonal river, blueprint margin, vertical strip, night atlas
- paper tone: warm gray, ash gray, blue gray, green gray
- main photo geometry: broad organic tear, narrow vertical shard, torn oval, angular city slice
- linework: maps, architecture, snowflake, measurement, crosshair, dotted orbit
- botanical trace: pine, willow, blossom, bare branch, pressed flower, seed grass
- accent light: amber lamp, white sparkle, brass dot, red ornament, phone glow

Keep the whole batch recognizably one style: flat scan, torn fiber edges, matte paper, small microtext, and quiet archival mood.
