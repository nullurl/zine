# Prompt Recipes

## Five-Paragraph Base Prompt

```text
Tall vertical [ratio] experimental poster on [fog white / pale gray / washed blue / blush silver] paper, 55%-80% low-information atmospheric field, subtle fibers, scan streaks, toner noise, and soft emulsion variation. Flat printed artifact, no black presentation frame, UI, mockup, glossy gradient, or shadow.

One spectral anchor: [eye / hand / face fragment / standing figure / cloud / tree / bird / crowd / object] rendered at [10%-35%] opacity through [frosted blur / halftone / negative / toner bloom / motion smear / xerox]. It occupies [12%-35%] of the page and remains embedded in the paper rather than floating as a framed photo.

Typography uses [primary display layer], [secondary expressive layer], and [microtype system]. Concentrate exact copy in [top / left / bottom] zone, allow one overlap or crop, and keep 35%-55% of the page free of high-contrast text. Exact text: [short user-supplied deck].

Add up to two support systems: [orbit / route / calendar / timeline / nodes / side label / translucent block / image strip]. Use one [signal color] in tiny ticks, one phrase, or one narrow rule, occupying less than 2% of the canvas. Add xerox grain, vellum transparency, low-resolution raster, toner dropout, and slight print misregistration.

Avoid Y2K chrome, rave flyer, neon, black background, glossy photography, smooth gradients, 3D, fashion campaign, dense font soup, fake technical data, UI, QR code, sponsor wall, copied names, logos, signatures, watermarks, and unrelated decorative motifs.
```

## Transparency Manifesto

Best for transparency, silence, water, memory, and perception.

- field: washed white and icy blue
- anchor: liquid or flower shadow at 15% opacity
- type: large handwritten/display word crossing the top
- support: bold grotesk subwords and one microtext manifesto footer
- signal: icy cyan or graphite, with one tiny black symbol

Keep the lower two-thirds mostly pale.

## Ghost Program Stack

Best for exhibitions, performances, releases, and festivals.

- field: blush gray or silver
- anchor: faint eye, face, or room texture
- type: repeated large event names at top in heavy grotesk, faded copies behind
- support: vertical edge label and one small image window
- signal: pale pink ticks or a rust side mark

## Route Calendar

Best for tours, seasons, months, locations, and timelines.

- field: blue-gray or warm white
- anchor: one black silhouette pair or photocopied tree
- type: month names and locations around a circular route
- support: nodes, arcs, tiny date numbers, one side botanical contour
- signal: warm cream, coral, or cobalt node

## Blurred Sense Catalog

Best for seeing, feeling, memory, body, and design research.

- field: clean-ish off-white with toner specks
- anchor: 2-3 small frosted sensory crops of one subject only
- type: one custom distorted glyph band across upper middle
- support: narrow word columns, barcode-like line block, large date footer
- signal: muted rust or pink-brown blur

## Spectral Figure Specimen

Best for type families, bodies, fashion research, and movement.

- split field: black specimen column and gray image field
- anchor: pale standing figure rendered as toner bloom
- type: mono weight table and one expressive degraded word
- support: one or two orbital lines crossing both halves
- signal: near-monochrome or one cold white/cyan point

## Album Press Sheet

Best for albums, tracks, essays, and film/music releases.

- field: washed blue, green-gray, or faded cream
- anchor: one atmospheric figure or wing-like cloud
- type: large serif title in lower middle, letterspaced artist/project name at center
- support: one narrow color image strip and bottom tracklist columns
- signal: muted red, navy, or cream

## Regeneration Recipes

### Too empty

Add: `one spectral edge reaches 30% opacity, one bold title reaches solid ink, and one tiny signal mark is clearly visible; all remaining field stays pale`.

### Too dense

Add: `exactly one title, one expressive word, one microtext band, one ghost image, and one diagram; remove all other text and icons`.

### Too glossy

Add: `cheap photocopy, toner bloom, uneven paper tooth, low-resolution raster, flat scanner capture, no smooth gradient or polished photo`.

### Copy is garbled

Shorten the exact deck, reserve a clear type zone, and reduce decorative type layers. Use placeholder microtext only when exact wording is not required.
