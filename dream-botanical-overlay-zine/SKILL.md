# Dream Botanical Overlay Zine

Turn the user's content into both:

1. a final image-generation prompt, and
2. a generated raster image made from that prompt.

## Source Grammar

Use this visual identity:

- vertical 3:5 or 4:5 analog photo-zine poster
- mobile-photo collage feeling, as if built with blend modes, transparency, and soft erasure
- translucent flower close-ups over landscapes, skies, water, rooftops, roads, or distant mountains
- bird flocks, bare tree branches, utility wires, eyes, and glowing human silhouettes as memory overlays
- split-frame structures: upper/lower panels, two-image stacks, or a dominant full-frame overlay with a horizontal ground band
- washed pink, blue, teal, lavender, cream, grey, and black silhouettes; high exposure blooms and soft fog
- visible film grain, dust, scratches, scan noise, imperfect edges, faded contrast, light leaks
- when a human face or body appears, keep it photographic and believable: Fujifilm or Ricoh GR-like candid color, natural skin tone, soft matte skin, mild asymmetry, real hair strands, no waxy AI face, no beauty-filter smoothing, no crunchy fake pore texture
- poetic solitude, afterimage, adolescent memory, seaside distance, spring flowers, dreamlike urban nature

The style should look like an emotional analog photo collage, not a clean botanical poster and not a paper stationery layout.

## Mode Policy

Use **Standard Mode** for all generation. Use the Standard Mode Prompt Compiler below to convert the user's content into compact, imageable prompts.

Use prompt-only output only when the user explicitly asks for prompt-only.

## Standard Mode Prompt Compiler

Default generation should compile only visible image instructions.

### First-Principles Fields

Every prompt must answer these rendering questions in this order:

1. **Canvas:** What is the frame?
   - tall vertical 3:5 or 4:5 phone-poster; analog photo print; full-frame or split-frame; no mockup; no UI unless the user explicitly asks to show editing process.

2. **Base Scene:** What photographic world sits underneath?
   - choose one main scene from the user's content: sea horizon, mountain valley, dusk street, rooftop, field, garden, beach, road, pale sky, bedroom-window view, or quiet urban edge.

3. **Botanical Overlay:** What flower or plant becomes the translucent emotional layer?
   - large soft-focus lily, tulip, daisy, rhododendron, branch blossom, fern, leaf vein, grass shadow, or garden flower field; let it partially cover the scene with screen/lighten/double-exposure blending.

4. **Memory Motif:** What secondary photographic motif sharpens the idea?
   - choose one: bird flock, bare tree branch silhouette, utility wires, overexposed human silhouette, walking figure, giant eye overlay, distant building, glowing horizon, black foreground strip, or red sun.
   - if the motif is a visible person rather than a silhouette, render them as a real candid photograph: Ricoh GR everyday snapshot, Fujifilm Classic Chrome or Classic Negative color, natural skin texture, soft optical focus, and no AI-retouched face.

5. **Composition Structure:** How are images layered?
   - choose full-frame double exposure, upper/lower split panel, stacked two-photo zine, landscape band behind flowers, black ground band with tiny figure, or collage of two rectangular photo windows.

6. **Blend Treatment:** What edit-process makes it belong to this style?
   - use translucent overlay, linear dodge, screen blend, soft eraser edge, high exposure bloom, blurred mask, low-opacity ghost image, slight misregistration, or layered film scan.

7. **Color Logic:** What palette carries the mood?
   - pink flowers against cyan/blue sky; blue ocean with peach sand; lavender sky with black branches; pale cream haze with faded teal mountains; graphite silhouettes with red-orange sun; low-to-medium contrast and washed color.

8. **Texture:** What analog damage is visible?
   - film grain, dust, scratches, light leak, scanner noise, faded ink, slight blur, chromatic bleed, compression-like softness, worn dark band. Keep grain restrained on faces; it should not become dirty AI speckle or fake pore texture.

9. **Typography:** Should text appear?
   - default to no text. If text is requested, use one tiny lowercase word or small date-like caption, low contrast, placed near an edge. Do not add poster headlines.

10. **Emotional Temperature:** What should the viewer feel first?
   - dream, distance, fragile memory, spring melancholy, afterimage, solitude, longing, soft surrealism, quiet cinematic diary.

11. **Hard Avoids:** What must not appear?
   - clean product ad, glossy mockup, crisp digital collage, UI controls, stickers, scrapbook clutter, cute illustration, anime, hard 3D, neon, fashion campaign, text-heavy design.

### Standard Prompt Shape

Write the final Standard Mode prompt as four compact paragraphs:

1. canvas + analog photo surface + base scene + overall composition
2. botanical overlay + memory motif + blend mode/treatment
3. color palette + grain/damage + optional sparse text
4. mood + flat/photographic constraints + avoid-list

Use specific nouns and placement. Do not describe the skill or reference images; describe only the image to generate.

## Variation Engine

Before writing the prompt, choose one option from each axis. Vary the image structure, not only the subject.

### Layout Family

- **full-flower-landscape:** one large translucent flower over a distant landscape
- **upper-lower-memory:** top photo panel and bottom photo panel stacked with a hard horizontal seam
- **black-ground-walker:** blue or pink botanical projection above a dark foreground band with one small silhouette
- **bird-branch-overlay:** pale branch network with birds layered over a second memory scene
- **eye-sea-double:** giant low-opacity eye blended over ocean, beach, or sky
- **utility-flower-sky:** flower close-up blended with utility poles, wires, clouds, and blue sky
- **pink-leaf-horizon:** flower or leaf-vein texture over a horizon with shrub silhouettes
- **contact-sheet-process:** clean white contact-sheet of source photos plus one or two dark edit-preview panels, only when the user asks for process/reference-board style

## Workflow

1. Parse the user's content.
   - Identify the central mood, subject, place, and any requested flower/person/memory.
   - If the user supplies a reference photo, treat it as the base scene, botanical overlay, or memory motif depending on its content.
   - If the theme is abstract, translate it into a concrete image pair: one place plus one botanical overlay plus one memory motif.

2. Select a variation recipe.
   - Choose layout, base scene, botanical overlay, memory motif, blend treatment, and palette from the Variation Engine.
   - Keep the composition emotionally legible. Two main images plus one motif are enough.
   - Use contact-sheet or visible phone-edit UI only when explicitly requested; otherwise generate the finished collage, not the editing interface.

3. Write the final image prompt.
   - Use the Standard Prompt Shape.
   - Specify the seam, bands, overlay scale, and blend behavior.
   - Make flowers large and translucent when they are the emotional layer.
   - If a person is visible, add camera-realism wording: candid Fujifilm/Ricoh color, natural skin tone, soft matte skin, real hair texture, no waxy face, no oily highlights, no crunchy AI noise.
   - Avoid adding text unless requested.

4. Generate the image.
   - Use the available image generation capability by default.
   - Do not stop after prompt-only unless the user asks for prompt-only.
   - If the result becomes a normal flower photo, normal landscape, or clean poster, tighten the prompt and regenerate once with stronger `double exposure`, `screen blend`, `film scan`, and `split-panel` wording.

5. Return the image and prompt.

## Negative Constraints

Always avoid:

- normal realistic botanical macro photo with no overlay
- clean travel landscape with no double exposure
- product ad, logo, CTA, commercial poster hierarchy
- glossy mockup, sharp vector graphics, clean digital collage
- phone UI, app controls, crop handles, or screenshots unless explicitly requested
- cute cartoon, anime, kawaii sticker, scrapbook decoration
- dense paper ephemera, washi tape, stationery collage
- high-fashion editorial, luxury perfume campaign, cinematic VFX
- neon cyberpunk, high-chroma gradient background, 3D rendering
- long readable typography or large slogan
- sterile white UI layout unless generating a contact-sheet process reference
- waxy AI face, plastic skin, oily facial highlights, over-sharpened pores, fake skin texture, or beauty-ad retouch when a person appears

## Quality Gate

Before finalizing, check:

- Did the run use the Standard Mode Prompt Compiler?
- Did the run choose a recipe across layout, base scene, botanical overlay, memory motif, blend treatment, and palette?
- Is the image a vertical analog photo-zine poster?
- Is there a clear double-exposure or overlay relationship?
- Is a flower, plant, branch, leaf, or botanical texture visually important?
- Is there a photographic base scene such as sea, sky, mountain, street, rooftop, field, or garden?
- Is at least one memory motif present when the recipe calls for it?
- Does the image include film grain, dust, scratches, faded contrast, light leak, or scan noise?
- If a person appears, do they read as a real candid photo with natural skin and camera color rather than AI beauty texture?
- Does the palette match washed pink-blue, lavender-black, teal-coral, cream-sage, or similar analog colors?
- Did the prompt avoid clean commercial poster, UI screenshot, dense scrapbook, cartoon, neon, 3D, and text-heavy aesthetics?
- Did you actually generate the image?
