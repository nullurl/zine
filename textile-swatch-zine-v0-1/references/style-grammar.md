# Textile Swatch Zine Style Grammar

Use this file when a request asks to reverse-engineer a reference, merge visual grammars, or correct style drift.

## Reverse-Engineered Reference Grammar

The target reference language is a quiet material archive:

- vertical off-white fibrous paper background
- upper-half 2x3 grid of translucent, frosted, slightly rounded sample cards
- real material specimens on cards: black coarse woven wool, ivory loop yarn, ice-blue satin, beige knit, handmade paper, or pale thread
- lower row may include one material card, one centered material-name card, and one vertical keyword card
- tiny black sans or sample-index type
- soft shadows, shallow relief, matte scan lighting, flat overhead/product-archive camera
- large untouched lower paper field
- bespoke sample-book mood, not a retail catalog

## Minimal Zine Fusion

Bring from Minimal Zine:

- blank paper is active composition, not leftover space
- one small concentrated attention structure
- restrained, poetic text
- one high-chroma or clear accent
- scan noise, paper fibers, old-print softness
- low-to-medium contrast and quiet editorial tone

Replace from Minimal Zine:

- do not use a single isolated tiny object as the whole poster
- do not rely on fragmented letters as the only visual anchor
- do not make the color accent only a tiny registration dot

The swatch-board system is the new anchor. It should occupy roughly 25%-45% of the page, usually above center, while the lower field remains quiet.

## Physical Construction

Make material samples believable by specifying:

- fibers: coarse wool, boucle yarn, brushed cotton, raw silk, satin, linen, rib knit, mesh, felt, handmade paper, soft leather
- edges: frayed selvage, deckled paper, cut felt, lifted fabric corner, loose thread tail, stitched tab
- surface: pile, loop, rib, weave, sheen, embossing, grain, small wrinkles
- depth: 1-3 mm lift, shallow contact shadow, no heavy theatrical shadow
- attachment: tucked under frosted card, pinned with tiny stitch, clipped by a narrow label strip, or taped with matte translucent tape

Avoid vague words like "texture samples" alone. Name the material and its surface behavior.

## Composition Ratios

- Canvas: 2:3 or 3:4 portrait.
- Paper field: 55%-75% visible background.
- Swatch system: 25%-45% of the canvas.
- Accent: 1%-8% of the page or one card.
- Text: less than 5% of page; tiny but intentionally placed.

Default placement: grid starts around 12%-18% from the top, centered horizontally, with a large lower blank field.

## Light And Camera

Use flat-lay or orthographic scan language:

- soft diffuse daylight
- matte paper fibers
- low-to-medium contrast
- shallow contact shadows
- slight print wear, dust, scan noise
- no deep perspective, no macro bokeh, no cinematic spotlight

## Style Drift Corrections

If the output becomes UI:

- replace "cards" with "frosted translucent sample slips made of paper"
- add "irregular fabric edges, thread tails, shallow physical contact shadows"
- add "not app cards, not digital UI panels"

If the output becomes retail:

- remove "color chart", "catalog", "product", "brand"
- add "bespoke studio archive, unbranded material ledger, fictional index labels"

If the output becomes too dark:

- specify "bright off-white paper, airy daylight, pale gray shadows, high-key material photography"
- keep black only as one textile sample or type color

If the output becomes too scrapbook-like:

- reduce photos, stickers, tickets, handwriting, lace, and decoration
- return to a disciplined six-cell material grid

If material looks flat:

- name the tactile structure of each sample
- add lifted edges, frayed fibers, knit ribs, satin fold highlights, looped yarn, and paper embossing
