# Prompt Recipes

Use these as compact layout patterns.

## Center Specimen

Use when the user wants the most minimal result.

- one tiny grayscale object or photo near center
- wide white paper field
- one short title in black
- optional small date or index

## Lower Note

Use when the subject feels like a quiet caption or memory.

- anchor sits low on the page
- title above or slightly left
- small metadata line beneath
- lots of empty paper above

## Upper Corner Card

Use when the theme benefits from an editorial label.

- small image block in one upper corner
- drifted caption text nearby
- faint microtext or numbering
- untouched blank field elsewhere

## Double Fragment

Use when the theme has a contrast or pair.

- two small grayscale fragments
- narrow gap between them
- one shared title
- both fragments stay tiny

## Prompt Shape

Keep the final prompt in four parts:

1. frame and paper
2. anchor and placement
3. typography and microtext
4. texture, scan logic, and avoid-list
