# Style Grammar

## Reference Deconstruction

The reference is a tall portrait sheet made from five landscape photographs. The page is not a conventional collage: it is a measured vertical archive of panoramic windows.

### Geometry

- Overall page is approximately 3:5, with a nearly white background.
- Five full-width horizontal windows occupy most of the page height.
- Every window has the same large rounded corner radius.
- White gutters are narrow but clearly visible and evenly repeated.
- There is no decorative border, shadow, tape, caption block, or mockup.
- Each crop is much wider than tall, so horizon lines read as calm bands.

### Visual Sequence

The reference moves from atmospheric to terrestrial:

1. A warm towering cloud over a dark tree line.
2. A brighter cloud mass interrupted by utility poles and wires.
3. A dark blue night field with a small moon and bird.
4. A hazy garden or lawn with trees, house, and simple structure.
5. Layered agricultural fields with horses and a low road.

This gives the page a quiet downward movement: sky -> infrastructure -> night -> lived garden -> ground and animals.

### Image Behavior

- Long-lens or cropped landscape photographs, not illustrations.
- Distant subjects and low horizons create large areas of sky or terrain.
- Cloud structures are detailed but not hyperreal or fantasy-like.
- Utility wires, poles, house roofs, fences, roads, and animals act as scale markers.
- The third strip is intentionally sparse; one moon and one bird carry the frame.
- Slight softness, film grain, muted contrast, and warm highlights unify different moments.

### Palette

- Main colors: pale sky blue, cloud cream, muted sage, dark blue-gray, grass green.
- Small temperature changes are allowed: warm cloud, cool night, misty gray garden, earthy field.
- Saturation is present but controlled. The set is not sepia and not washed into gray.
- Avoid a different grading identity in each strip.

## Minimal Zine Fusion

From Minimal Zine, keep:

- a strict page geometry
- a single repeated visual system
- active white space and controlled rhythm
- sparse or absent typography
- one restrained color logic
- archival/printed softness

Change:

- tiny paper cluster -> five panoramic landscape windows
- isolated object -> sequence of environmental observations
- one small chromatic anchor -> coherent natural color family
- static paper specimen -> time, distance, and weather progression

## Failure Diagnosis

### It looks like a photo grid template

Specify a narrow narrative order, shared lens character, consistent horizon behavior, and one place or weather memory. Remove generic captions and decorative separators.

### The strips look like rounded cards

Remove shadows, UI spacing language, borders, and card effects. Say `flat printed sheet with photographs embedded in white page, no interface, no mockup`.

### The result is one landscape with dividers

Describe five distinct photographic moments with independent exposure, focal subject, and horizon. Require white page gaps and visible corner radii around each strip.

### Color drifts

Choose one palette family and repeat its film treatment in every strip. Allow only a controlled time-of-day shift.

### The page is too busy

Keep each strip to one anchor and one scale marker. Preserve the sparse moon strip and avoid adding text or stickers.
