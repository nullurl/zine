# Prompt Recipes

## Standard Prompt Shape

```text
Tall vertical 3:5 white editorial page, five equal-width panoramic landscape photo windows stacked vertically, consistent large rounded corners, narrow even white gutters, no borders, no shadows, no mockup.

Strip 1: [sky or cloud event]. Strip 2: [horizon or infrastructure event]. Strip 3: [sparse night or weather event]. Strip 4: [garden, house, or lived place]. Strip 5: [field, road, water, or animal event]. The sequence follows [time/distance/emotional order], each strip independent but belonging to one location and memory.

Cohesive [palette] with soft natural light, analog landscape photography, gentle film grain, slight lens softness, muted contrast, matte printed texture, realistic scale and atmosphere. Add only [short title/date] in tiny type on the outer margin, or no text.

No scrapbook stickers, tape, polaroid borders, interface cards, fake long text, commercial travel brochure, HDR, oversaturation, purple grading, teal-orange grading, CGI, 3D, fantasy clouds, impossible moon scale, watermarks, or logos.
```

## Sky-to-Ground Diary

Use for summer, weather, home, rural memory, and a day observed from one window.

- Strip 1: towering cream cloud above a dark tree line.
- Strip 2: cloud bank crossed by power poles and wires.
- Strip 3: deep blue night with one small moon and one bird silhouette.
- Strip 4: misty garden, low house, trees, and open lawn.
- Strip 5: layered field, low road, a few horses or distant figures.

Keep the middle night strip visually sparse so the page can breathe.

## Day-to-Night Sequence

Use a fixed place seen across time.

1. warm late-afternoon sky
2. blue-hour infrastructure or rooftops
3. dark moonlit interval
4. cool early-morning garden
5. pale field after rain

Do not make every strip a sunset. Change the light while keeping the lens and color family stable.

## Weather Contact Sheet

Use for rain, wind, monsoon, cloud watching, or seasonal change.

- repeat the same broad horizon or location
- change only cloud density, moisture, visibility, or ground reflection
- preserve the same rounded strip architecture
- use a quiet title such as `weather notes` or an exact user-supplied phrase

## Near-to-Far Memory

Use for travel, leaving home, a road, or an unnamed place.

- foreground: soft plant or window reflection
- middle distance: house, garden, poles, or road
- far distance: ridge, sea, cloud, or moon
- final strip: tiny animal, vehicle, or human scale marker

## Regeneration Recipes

### Rounded geometry failed

Add: `exactly five separate panoramic photographs, each full width with identical 28px-style rounded corners, clearly visible white gutters between strips, flat white page`.

### Photos became illustrations

Add: `real photographic exposure, natural lens behavior, documentary landscape scale, subtle analog film grain, no painterly brushwork, no concept art`.

### Too colorful

Add: `one coherent blue-green and cream film palette, restrained saturation, natural warm highlights only, no independent color grade per strip`.

### Too generic

Replace generic words with exact anchors: `cumulonimbus above tree line`, `utility poles crossing cloud bank`, `small moon with one bird`, `low house in wet lawn`, `horses in layered field`.

### Text is distorted

Remove all text or limit it to one short title of 2-5 words and one small date line placed in the white margin.
