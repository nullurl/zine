# Prompt Recipes

## Five-Paragraph Base Prompt

```text
Tall vertical 3:5 matte off-white paper page, exactly five equal-width panoramic windows stacked vertically, identical large rounded corners, narrow even white gutters, quiet outer margins, flat scanned-paper view, no borders, shadows, card UI, or mockup.

Cell 1: [photo landscape anchor]. Cell 2: [hand-drawn study or photo-plus-pencil trace]. Cell 3: [photo landscape anchor]. Cell 4: [sparse map, watercolor observation, or ink notation]. Cell 5: [photo landscape anchor]. The cells follow [time, distance, weather, or emotional order] and belong to one place-memory.

Apply Minimal Zine color logic: subdued paper, grayscale support, one opaque high-chroma [hue] appearing as [pencil mark, field detail, color block, or subject], occupying about 0.8%-2.5% of the page or 15%-35% of the active cluster, visible at thumbnail size.

Hand-drawn matter uses [medium] with broken contours, uneven pressure, erased edges, paper fibers, tiny arrows or labels, and slight registration drift. Every mark traces or annotates a photographed feature; no decorative cartoon doodles, no dense scrapbook. Use soft natural light, analog landscape photography, gentle film grain, xerox/riso softness, matte paper tooth, and low-to-medium contrast.

Optional tiny [short phrase/date/location] in restrained serif or typewriter text in the white margin. Avoid full-bleed scenery, stickers, tape, commercial travel design, glossy 3D, neon, purple grading, HDR, long text, logos, watermarks, copied signatures, and unreadable clutter.
```

## Cloud / Moon / Garden / Field

Use five cells:

1. warm cumulus cloud above a dark tree line, real photograph
2. graphite cloud contour and tiny weather note on paper
3. deep blue night photograph with a small moon and one bird
4. translucent watercolor garden study with house roof and tree line
5. green layered field photograph with horses and a low road

Use `cloud cream and sky blue` with one cobalt pencil accent.

## Weather Notebook

Use four or six cells. Repeat one broad horizon while changing only cloud density, wind, rain haze, wet reflection, and visibility. Alternate photo, pencil, photo, ink notation, photo, watercolor. Keep the drawings sparse and observational.

## Route Journal

Use five cells:

1. foreground plant or window reflection photo
2. hand-drawn route line and compass fragment
3. distant house or utility line photo
4. small watercolor elevation study
5. field, coast, road, or animal photo

Use `field green with warm earth` and one tomato-red or cobalt route mark.

## Minimal Zine Anchor Variants

- cobalt blue pencil contour around one cloud edge
- ultramarine rectangular swatch in a quiet paper gutter
- pear-green ink trace over a distant field boundary
- lemon-yellow weather symbol beside the moon cell
- tomato-red roof or tiny route dot, kept opaque and isolated

Never reduce the selected hue to a barely visible registration tick. Do not add a second unrelated accent family.

## Regeneration Recipes

### Not enough hand drawing

Require: `cells 2 and 4 are visibly hand-drawn on the same paper stock, with graphite pressure variation, erased edges, one short handwritten note, and no photographic texture inside those two cells`.

### Too much hand drawing

Require: `only cells 2 and 4 contain linework, each limited to one observed feature; cells 1, 3, and 5 remain photographic and clean`.

### All cells look identical

Assign a treatment label to every cell and state the narrative order. Vary distance or time, not the page geometry.

### Paper disappeared

Add: `visible off-white page gutters and margins, matte absorbent paper surrounding every strip, flat scanned artifact, no edge-to-edge landscape`.

### Color is weak

Add: `fully saturated opaque cobalt pencil mark, clearly visible at thumbnail scale, approximately 2% of total page, all other colors restrained`.
