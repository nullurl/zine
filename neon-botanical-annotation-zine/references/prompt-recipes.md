# Neon Botanical Annotation Zine Prompt Recipes

Use this file when selecting a layout, translating a theme, or producing variations.

## Recipe Matrix

### flower-callout

Use for: one flower name, a quiet plant portrait, or a field-note look.

Ingredients:

- one large photographic flower or stem
- one or two leader lines
- script or serif plant name
- a few circles or dots
- subdued dark background

### numbered-neon-field

Use for: street plant, memory route, playful Y2K annotation, or a reference like numbered magenta/cyan marks.

Ingredients:

- foreground flower or vine
- magenta numbered circles
- cyan squares and star marks
- one curved cyan line or trail
- short curved title or note

### framed-specimen

Use for: daisies, small wildflowers, grasses, gentle field notes.

Ingredients:

- thin white rectangular frame around the main flower group
- translucent green or pale circles
- small caption near the lower edge
- dark foliage as negative space

### pixel-botany

Use for: lily, herb, small flower, retro web, or botanical index moods.

Ingredients:

- real flower photo on the right or center
- white pixel-art line drawing on the left
- dotted matrix in one corner
- one plant-name label
- one red or magenta circular mark

### night-bouquet-labels

Use for: lilies, chamomile, bouquet, black background, melancholic night.

Ingredients:

- flowers emerging from black space
- pale blue dots drifting through the image
- thin white label lines
- script plant labels and tiny parenthetical notes

### color-chip-garden

Use for: grass, bottle, object in garden, palette study, design note.

Ingredients:

- real grass/object photo
- translucent panel with 3-5 color chips
- thin white frame boxes
- tiny italic title
- small stars or dots

### sky-note-flower

Use for: summer, sunlight, blue sky, music, simple joy.

Ingredients:

- one silhouetted flower against sky
- pale circular notes and music staff
- tiny stars
- small inset card in one corner
- soft sky as negative space

## Theme Translation

For "lily":

- dark garden, red or white lily anchor, script title "Lily", white leader lines, red dots or pale blue dots

For "pink flower":

- shaded street or garden, pink flower foreground, cyan/magenta numbered marks, tiny curved note

For "daisy" or "wildflower":

- grass field, thin white rectangular frame, translucent green circles, tiny serif caption

For "summer":

- bright sky, one flower silhouette, cream/powder-blue dots, small inset card, tiny title "summer days"

For "night":

- black background, pale flower, blue dots, script plant labels, minimal white leader lines

For "grass palette":

- dark grass photograph, glass bottle or leaf anchor, translucent color-chip panel, white frames

## Prompt Skeleton

Use this structure and replace bracketed fields:

```text
Portrait [2:3 or 3:5] [or wide 16:10 if requested] botanical photograph, [setting], [lighting], [negative-space zone], film grain and soft scan/screen texture.

Plant anchor: [species or visual form], [scale and position], [focus treatment], [background depth and mood].

[Layout family] overlay system: [mark types and counts], [positions], [route], [frames/arrows/dots/chips], composited like early-digital zine annotations rather than a functional UI.

Typography: title "[SHORT TITLE]", [font voice], [placement], [tiny note if any], no long copy, no copied reference captions, no brand names.

Palette and texture: [photo palette], [accent colors], [glow level], [grain/halftone/screen softness], [misregistration or imperfect placement].

Mood and constraints: [emotional temperature]; avoid clean UI, social template, polished scrapbook, full vector flowers, heavy cyberpunk glow, long text, watermarks, brands, and identifiable private people.
```

## Title Bank

Use short original titles:

- Night Lily
- Flower Note
- Summer Field
- Garden Signal
- Little Bloom
- Dark Garden
- Pink Route
- Grass Palette
- Wild Index
- After Rain

Keep title text short because image models distort long text.
