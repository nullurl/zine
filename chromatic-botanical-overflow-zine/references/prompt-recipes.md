# Prompt Recipes

Use one recipe as a starting point. Replace the plant, exact text, and field color with the user's brief. Do not copy visible reference text or exact flower arrangements.

## Contents

- Six-paragraph base prompt
- Recipes A-E
- Copy and palette rules
- Regeneration corrections

## Six-Paragraph Base Prompt

```text
Tall vertical [3:5 / 4:5 / 2:3] botanical editorial poster on one flat solid [field color] printed paper background, 65%-88% quiet color field, matte scanned surface, subtle paper tooth, no gradient, no room, no desk, no mockup, no black border.

Place exactly one slightly offset warm-white instant-photo frame, [frame width] by [frame height], with [border thickness] white border and a blank lower caption strip. The inner photo window shows [plant subject] in a softly photographed [season/light/background] scene. Use one frame only, no rounded corners, no frame stack, no sticker grid, no extra cards.

Make one continuous [branch/stem/flower group] cross the frame boundary in the [overflow direction]. The plant must continue anatomically from the inner photo into the exterior cutout, extending across [one/two] frame edges and occupying [12%-35%] of the canvas. The exterior petals/leaves are sharper and slightly foregrounded but share the same species, light direction, and color as the framed plant.

Lock the palette by role: [field hue and approximate hex], neutral warm-white frame, [petal/leaf colors], [stem/shadow color], and [center accent]. Keep the field flat and saturated, the frame neutral, and the plant naturally separated. Do not globally tint, desaturate, blend into a gradient, or mix unrelated palettes.

Use [no text / exact short text] in the blank caption strip with small [serif/typewriter/monospaced] lettering. Add soft contact shadow only where the plant crosses the frame, mild paper fibers, low-resolution raster softness, slight edge wear, and diffuse studio daylight. No logo, author, signature, watermark, date, or other invented text unless supplied.

Mood: [spring / tender / graphic / quiet / botanical / optimistic]. Avoid Polaroid stacks, scrapbook clutter, tickets, stickers, washi tape, product advertising, glossy 3D frames, hard shadows, CGI, UI cards, stock moodboards, unrelated plants, malformed stems, rainbow colors, gradients, and long paragraphs.
```

## Recipe A: Blush Blossom Overflow

Mode: Blossom Overflow.

- field: dusty rose or muted pink
- frame: slightly upper-right, large white border, blank lower strip
- photo: soft pink cherry or peach blossom cluster
- overflow: dark branch enters from lower-left and rises across the frame; blossoms cross the lower-left border
- palette: rose field, pale pink petals, brown stem, warm white frame, tiny yellow pollen centers

Use for spring, tenderness, first bloom, and quiet optimism.

## Recipe B: Pine Lotus Cutout

Mode: Blossom Overflow or Color-Field Herbarium.

- field: deep forest green
- frame: centered, photo window filled with dark lotus leaves or pond texture
- overflow: one large magenta lotus crosses the top border and projects into the field
- palette: pine field, magenta/purple petals, teal-green leaves, white frame, warm yellow center

Use for tropical plants, lotus, water, ritual, and saturated botanical studies.

## Recipe C: Sky Branch

Mode: Single Stem Specimen.

- field: medium sky blue or pale periwinkle
- frame: lower-middle, generous paper border
- photo: white blossom branch against blue sky
- overflow: thin branch rises from the lower frame edge and exits above the frame
- palette: blue field, white petals, green buds, charcoal branch, warm white frame

Use for lightness, distance, early spring, sky, and minimal botanical identity.

## Recipe D: Sage Magnolia

Mode: Frame and Counterbranch.

- field: muted sage or deep pine
- frame: slightly right-shifted with blurred green background inside
- photo: white magnolia branch in soft natural light
- overflow: large white flower cluster crosses the lower-left frame edge
- palette: sage/pine field, white petals, olive buds, brown branch, neutral frame

Use for calm, garden memory, white flowers, and understated natural color.

## Recipe E: Mustard Tree Field

Mode: Palette-Locked Reference.

- field: flat mustard yellow
- frame: centered, photo window showing one tree beside water or a distant blue-gray horizon
- overflow: dark branching canopy grows beyond the top and side of the frame
- palette: mustard field, charcoal tree, blue-gray water/sky, green foliage, white frame
- text: none by default

Use for graphic landscape botanicals, high-contrast trees, and bold color-field posters.

## Copy Rules

- Default to no text.
- If text is supplied, preserve it exactly and keep it to one short line.
- Do not invent a brand, studio, author, date, or signature.
- Keep the lower caption strip blank when the user asks for no text or no attribution.

## Regeneration Corrections

### Plant remains inside the frame

```text
the same continuous branch visibly crosses the white border in front and extends into the outer solid color field; the cutout is not clipped at the frame edge
```

### Field loses its color

```text
use one perfectly flat solid [exact hue family] printed background covering 75% of the canvas, no gradient, no room, no shadows, no global desaturation, no color wash
```

### Result becomes a card grid

```text
one single white-bordered photo frame only, no duplicate frames, no rounded cards, no Polaroid stack, no scrapbook fragments, no labels, no tickets, no stickers
```

### Cutout looks artificial

```text
match the interior and exterior plant by species, branch direction, light source, scale, and color; use clean slightly feathered edges, natural translucent petals, and one very soft contact shadow at the frame crossing
```

### Too much decoration

```text
remove all text, icons, tape, props, vases, packaging, and decorative marks; keep one plant, one frame, one field color, and a blank caption strip
```
