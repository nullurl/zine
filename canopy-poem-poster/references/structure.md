# Canopy Poem Poster Structure

## Reverse-engineered source DNA

- Large pale field with soft watercolor or mist-like atmosphere.
- Edges are framed by green canopy, cloud masses, foliage, or soft terrain.
- A broad open center remains mostly empty.
- Small markers such as people, cars, or path details add scale without taking over.
- The poem sits lightly in the clearing, often low or center-low.
- The mood is calm, spacious, and slightly dreamlike.

## Layout families

- **center-clearing:** canopy along borders, poem floating in the open middle.
- **edge-canopy:** stronger foliage on left/right/top edges, central void preserved.
- **diagonal-clear-path:** a diagonal opening crosses the composition and carries the text.
- **tiny-marker-field:** poem and tiny scale markers share the open field.
- **low-horizon-poem:** landscape sits low, with text above it in air.
- **border-ring-clearing:** soft clusters ring the page and leave a near-empty interior.

## Poem placement rules

- Use one short poem excerpt or a few short lines.
- Place the poem where the clearing is strongest.
- Keep type small, light, and restrained.
- Allow one larger anchor word only if it helps structure the empty field.
- Avoid dense paragraph setting or poster-style headline hierarchy.

## Accent and marker rules

- Use one restrained accent only: a pink car, yellow note, small blue marker, or similar.
- Tiny figures, path dots, or roadway hints can provide scale.
- Keep these markers secondary to the open clearing and poem.

## Texture rules

- Soft watercolor wash, low-contrast scan softness, paper fibers, gentle blur.
- Pale paper or sky field should remain dominant.
- No hard shadows, glossy edges, or high-contrast collage cutouts.

## Prompt recipe

1. Canvas and paper field: ratio, open-space ratio, atmospheric base.
2. Canopy and clearing: edge framing, open center, landscape softness.
3. Poem layout: small lines, placement, size, drift, or anchor word.
4. Markers and accent: tiny vehicles/figures, one color accent, texture.
5. Mood and avoids: airy literary tone plus hard constraints.

## Hard avoids

- Full-bleed landscape
- Dense scrapbook collage
- Commercial poster typography
- Heavy black calligraphy
- Bright neon or saturated multi-color chaos
- UI layout or mockup styling
- Busy background texture that kills the clearing

## Example prompt skeleton

```text
Tall vertical poster on pale paper or fog-soft sky, with 80% open space and gentle scan softness.

Soft green canopy masses frame the edges and corners, leaving a broad quiet clearing in the center for the poem.

Place three short poem lines lightly in the clearing, small and widely tracked; add one tiny accent marker such as a pink car or yellow note, if needed.

Keep it reflective and airy, with watercolor blur and paper grain; avoid commercial hierarchy, dense collage, heavy calligraphy, and full-bleed scenery.
```
