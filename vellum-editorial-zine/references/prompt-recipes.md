# Vellum Editorial Zine Prompt Recipes

Choose one recipe and replace the anchor, wording, surface, and accent with the user's brief. Keep the layer system stable.

## 1. Quiet Portrait Sheet

- Surface: cool gray paper or plaster
- Backing: white portrait sheet
- Anchor: one softly lit face or gesture, supplied by the user or generic and non-identifying
- Overlay: pale vellum across the lower middle
- Type: centered serif title and tiny monospaced descriptor
- Accent: oxblood label
- Mood: intimate, personal, restrained
- Title pattern: A Quiet Portrait

## 2. Café Insert

- Surface: deep forest green or dark teal paper
- Backing: warm white sheet
- Anchor: one café table, cup, pastry, or interior detail
- Overlay: cool gray translucent menu-like sheet
- Type: short script title plus two short category lines
- Accent: deep green or faded cobalt
- Mood: tactile, calm, lightly commercial but not an advertisement
- Title pattern: Afternoon Table

## 3. Material Study

- Surface: warm brown or charcoal board
- Backing: cream sheet
- Anchor: textile, paper, wood, metal, or shadow pattern
- Overlay: two pale sheets with different opacity, only if needed
- Type: small uppercase title and one short thesis line
- Accent: black or warm ivory
- Mood: abstract, archival, design-research oriented
- Title pattern: Matter in Quiet

## 4. Monthly Moments

- Surface: muted brown, olive, or charcoal
- Backing: white sheet with a small image near the upper-left
- Anchor: one object or photo fragment representing the month
- Overlay: large translucent sheet with the month title near center
- Type: title plus one short sentence, no dense list
- Accent: one muted color label
- Mood: diary-like, reflective, editorial
- Title pattern: APRIL NOTES

## 5. Architecture Portfolio

- Surface: foggy sage, pale gray, or concrete
- Backing: white sheet with one interior or exterior photograph
- Overlay: pale gray vellum crossing the lower third
- Type: elegant serif name for the concept, tiny descriptor below
- Accent: graphite black or faded cobalt
- Mood: spatial, calm, precise
- Title pattern: Soft Structures

## 6. Personal Image Essay

- Surface: gray or warm taupe
- Backing: one portrait-format print
- Anchor: hand, fabric, room corner, or object connected to a private memory
- Overlay: vellum carries a short first-person phrase
- Type: one editorial title and a two-line note
- Accent: warm burgundy mark
- Mood: vulnerable but not sentimental
- Title pattern: Things I Kept

## 7. Dark and Ivory

- Surface: nearly black brown or dark green
- Backing: bright cream paper
- Anchor: dark photograph or abstract black field with one quiet highlight
- Overlay: light gray vellum
- Type: ivory title and one small star or index mark
- Accent: ivory only, no extra saturated color
- Mood: nocturnal, crafted, controlled
- Title pattern: Made in Shadow

## Abstract Theme Translation

Translate abstract ideas into one visual object or surface:

- trust: a translucent sheet revealing a stable image beneath
- distance: an offset image partly hidden by vellum
- memory: a faded print with a clean paper border and small index
- silence: pale overlay, sparse type, no graphic decoration
- work: process detail, material sample, or quiet studio surface
- intimacy: hand, fabric, or close crop with limited text
- transition: two paper tones meeting under one translucent layer
- belonging: a framed image held by a clip and a short location line

## Targeted Corrections

### Vellum Disappears

State that the overlay is a large matte tracing-paper rectangle with visibly lighter opacity than the image, a distinct paper edge, and text printed on top.

### Looks Like Plastic

Remove glass, acrylic, glossy, translucent plastic, and strong reflections. Add fibrous tracing paper, matte surface, ink grain, and shallow contact shadow.

### Looks Like a Brand Brochure

Remove logo-like names, sales copy, CTA, pricing, and polished campaign hierarchy. Use a short poetic title, one factual descriptor, and private editorial intent.

### Too Much Text

Use one 2-5 word title, one short subline, and at most two tiny metadata lines. Let opacity and image layering carry the composition.

### Too Flat

Separate paper tones and offsets, add one thin metal clip, a subtle paper edge shadow, and slight rotation. Avoid deep perspective or floating 3D.

### Too Gray

Keep neutral paper and low contrast, but introduce one deliberate accent such as oxblood, deep green, faded cobalt, or warm ivory.

### Too Busy

Remove all but one image, one vellum sheet, one clip, and one accent label. Restore a broad quiet tabletop margin.
