# Botanical Scrapbook Zine Prompt Recipes

Use one recipe as a starting structure, then replace its subjects, text, and accent with details from the user's theme. Do not combine every recipe.

## 1. Sunlit Garden Walk

- Layout: drifting-clippings
- Anchor: wide canopy or path photograph
- Supports: leaves against light, flower cluster, hand with a found stem, one blurred walking frame
- Material: two clipped serif strips and one tiny handwritten date
- Accent: butter-yellow glints
- Mood: open, warm, quietly euphoric
- Useful title pattern: A Small Bright Day

## 2. Rain and White Flowers

- Layout: split-sunlight with a darker photo strip
- Anchor: white flowers in rain against deep foliage
- Supports: wet leaf, puddle rings, window drops, garden path
- Material: black frame fragment and one translucent note
- Accent: cyan outlined drops
- Mood: intimate, clean, reflective
- Useful title pattern: The Rain Stayed

## 3. Two-Person Memory

- Layout: central-polaroid-stack
- Anchor: hands, shared book, two shadows, or objects placed together; avoid generic posed portraits
- Supports: path, flower, table detail, light through leaves
- Material: ruled notebook sheet and three cut-paper words
- Accent: forest green or cobalt cut letters
- Mood: tender without wedding styling
- Useful title pattern: You, Here, Again

## 4. Sound of a Walk

- Layout: sound-and-leaf
- Anchor: open ring album with two botanical snapshots
- Supports: distant tree line and a small hand-held detail
- Material: sparse music notation crossing only the open paper field
- Accent: black notation plus one butter-yellow glint
- Mood: rhythmic, thoughtful, airy
- Useful title pattern: Leaves Keep Time

## 5. Keepsake Tin

- Layout: keepsake-tin
- Anchor: open shallow silver tin centered against a blurred garden or forest
- Supports inside the tin: one landscape print, two small Polaroids, one flower or book detail
- Material: lace or ribbon, tag, earphone cord or receipt; select only two
- Accent: one green quotation block or cyan line drops
- Mood: portable private archive
- Useful title pattern: Kept From Today

## 6. Field Ledger

- Layout: botanical-ledger
- Anchor: kraft folder or index board occupying the lower half
- Supports: contact strip, leaf close-up, path, water or grass
- Material: paper clip, tiny article clipping, one color swatch
- Accent: pale olive indexing squares
- Mood: observational, archival, youthful
- Useful title pattern: Notes for Growing

## 7. One Photograph, More Air

Use when the user asks for a sparse result.

- Layout: split-sunlight
- Anchor: one partial-bleed vertical botanical photograph
- Supports: one small framed detail only
- Material: one three-line clipped phrase
- Accent: black halftone specimen or eye
- Visible paper: 55%-75%
- Mood: poetic, restrained, slightly surreal

## 8. Seasonal Batch

For a four-image batch, rotate structure rather than only color:

1. spring: drifting-clippings / blossoms / butter-yellow glints
2. summer: open-ring-album / canopy / cobalt cut letters
3. autumn: botanical-ledger / seed heads / vermilion arrows
4. winter: split-sunlight / bare branch and pale sky / black halftone

Keep paper treatment and type logic consistent enough for the batch to feel related.

## Abstract Theme Translation

Translate abstract themes into evidence from a walk:

- hope: new leaf crossing hard sunlight
- waiting: empty path and unopened bud
- reunion: two shadows or two objects sharing one frame
- healing: hand near fresh growth, repaired page, recurring green
- memory: soft moving view plus one crisp specimen
- music: notation flowing through empty paper, not covering photographs
- time: contact strip, repeated tree shadow, small index numerals
- distance: path disappearing between trees and clipped text at opposite edge

## Targeted Corrections

### Too Dense

Increase visible paper to 55%, remove half the tertiary marks, keep one support material, and separate the two largest photographs.

### Too Much Like a Mood Board

State that every photograph comes from the same outing and film roll. Name the anchor, sequence, season, weather, and shared light.

### Too Digital

Strengthen fibrous paper, cut edges, printed strips, shallow tape shadows, scan noise, and imperfect alignment. Remove rounded cards and clean grid spacing.

### Too Dark

Request bright diffuse daylight, luminous botanical greens, warm white paper, lifted shadows, and no dark wash across the page.

### Text Dominates

Limit the title to 2-4 words, reduce it below the anchor-photo width, remove paragraph text, and retain only one tiny note.

### Looks Like an Advertisement

Remove headline hierarchy, logo-like lockups, product staging, CTA spacing, and polished brand copy. Reinforce private field-note language and imperfect snapshots.

### Too Many Colors

Return all paper and type to neutral black, ivory, or kraft. Keep natural photographic greens and one added accent only.
