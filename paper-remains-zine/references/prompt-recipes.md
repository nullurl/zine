# Prompt Recipes

Use these recipes when translating a theme into the remnant-paper system.

## Single Remnant

Use for one short phrase, object, or memory.

- one torn cluster in the middle or slightly low
- a short Chinese line near the cluster
- one accent block or seal

## Envelope Window

Use for notes, messages, memory fragments, or hidden text.

- torn envelope flap or paper window
- inner scrap, stamp, or folded note visible inside
- a small typed label near the edge

## Stamp Trace

Use for words, records, or conceptual residues.

- stamp circles, seals, or postmarks organize the composition
- one accent mark appears as a block or seal
- text remains short and secondary

## Split Cutout

Use for contrast, repair, or broken relation.

- two or three overlapping torn scraps
- one visible seam or repaired edge
- one accent shape bridging the fragments

## Margin Remainder

Use for quiet, spacious posters.

- most of the page stays empty
- remnants gather off-center
- small text drifts near the margins

## Block And Trace

Use when the accent needs to be stronger.

- one saturated block, arrow, or brush shape
- faint scraps and printed residue around it
- title stays small and calm

## Prompt Skeleton

```text
Portrait 3:4 sparse torn-paper relic poster, warm off-white fibrous paper, large blank field, flat scanned paper texture, matte surface, mild wear, no border or mockup.

One compact remnant cluster [placement], with torn paper scraps, tape seams, envelope edges, stamp circles, photocopy residue, or a recovered label, sized [size], with [accent color / form] as the only strong chromatic anchor.

Short Chinese title "[TITLE]" and optional tiny date or English line, printed or stamped, kept secondary and physically integrated into the paper.

Paper fibers, dust, subtle creases, shallow relief, worn edges, and soft print defects; avoid scrapbook clutter, postcard frames, social templates, UI cards, glossy 3D mockup, brand ads, long paragraphs, and copied text.
```
