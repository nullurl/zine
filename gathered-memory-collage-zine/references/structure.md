# Gathered Memory Collage Structure

## Reverse-engineered source DNA

- Large warm paper field with soft scan lighting.
- One central collage cluster, usually compact and slightly below center.
- 1-3 photo scraps with torn or deckled edges.
- Transparent or semi-transparent overlays: vellum, tracing paper, tape.
- Small supporting fragments: a second photo, a tiny stamp, a paper tab, a grid strip, or a faded label.
- Sparse hand-drawn marks: faint orbit curves, dotted arcs, x-marks, small circles, short rules.
- Tiny serif or typewriter captions only when needed.
- Overall mood: calm, diary-like, archival, touched by daylight.

## Layout families

- **center-stack:** one main vertical photo scrap, one or two smaller fragments, balanced around the middle.
- **offset-pile:** main scrap slightly left or right of center, with a smaller scrap to the opposite side.
- **paired-memory:** one scene scrap and one detail scrap stacked with a visible gap.
- **single-anchor:** one photo block plus tape, vellum, and one tiny secondary mark.
- **travel-board:** layered scraps with a horizon, window, or landscape anchor and faint route curves.

## Material recipes

- Warm ivory handmade paper, subtle fiber grain, low contrast.
- Torn white paper fragments, slightly shadowed.
- Translucent tape strips, pale beige or frosted clear.
- Faint graph paper, perforated notebook edges, or thin tracing-paper panels.
- Muted photo color with soft blacks and gentle highlights.

## Prompt recipe

1. Canvas and paper: ratio, paper tone, empty-space percentage, scan feel.
2. Memory anchor: subject, scrap count, crop shape, placement, tape/vellum.
3. Marks and typography: orbit lines, dotted paths, tiny labels, caption style.
4. Mood and avoids: quiet archival tone plus hard constraints.

## Good hard avoids

- Commercial poster hierarchy
- Dense scrapbook clutter
- Glossy tape or 3D paper mockup
- Neon or RGB glitch
- Full-bleed photo realism
- Large readable text blocks

## Example prompt skeleton

```text
Warm vertical paper poster with 80% empty ivory space and soft scan texture.

One main torn photo scrap showing the subject, plus one smaller supporting scrap, both layered with translucent tape.

Add one thin orbit line, a few tiny x-marks, and a small serif caption near the edge.

Keep it quiet, archival, and flat; avoid commercial layout, dense collage clutter, glossy mockup, neon, and full-bleed realism.
```
