# Prompt Recipes

Select one recipe and replace its subject slots. Do not copy sample labels when the user's content suggests better ones.

## Closed Guide Object

- State: closed-file
- Artifact: thick kraft guidebook or pressboard manual
- Layers: rounded cover, drilled holes, page block, index table
- Hardware: one large paper clip or ring fastener
- Information: small title, edition line, accession grid, barcode-like index
- Color: kraft + black ink + one faded red or cobalt mark
- Best for: brand systems, institutions, cities, exhibitions, manuals

## Open Pocket Binder

- State: open-dossier
- Artifact: open two-panel folder or ring binder
- Layers: left pocket, loose card, translucent report sheet, backing page
- Hardware: clip, two-hole ring, small rivets
- Information: cover label, page code, short history or field-note title
- Color: gray/kraft base + blush or powder-blue insert
- Best for: identity systems, research collections, personal archives

## Chromatic Experiment File

- State: chromatic-experiment-file
- Artifact: open working notebook with overlapping forms
- Layers: cream worksheet, cyan form, lemon instruction card, gray backing
- Hardware: rings, split pins, paper clips
- Information: numbered modules, boxes, diagrams, handwritten scoring
- Color: one dominant cyan or lemon family plus neutral paper
- Best for: process, design education, product research, experiments

## Single Evidence Report

- State: single-record-sheet
- Artifact: one vertical official-looking report sheet
- Layers: ruled form, one centered monochrome image, annotation margin
- Hardware: punched holes or one top clip
- Information: date, location, object label, stamp, short caption
- Color: ivory + gray-black type + faded red stamp
- Best for: objects, forensic concepts, inventions, technical studies

## Observation Sheet

- State: single-record-sheet
- Artifact: weather, cloud, botanical, astronomical, or material observation record
- Layers: mounted photo window, hand-drawn diagram window, measurement table
- Hardware: photo corners, small paper clip, punched margin
- Information: number, date, time, place, conditions, observer code
- Color: oxidized cream + graphite + one faded blue or red notation
- Best for: sky, seasons, plants, oceans, landscapes, science themes

## Ring-Bound Mission Log

- State: ring-bound-log
- Artifact: thick off-white mission book or field log
- Layers: cover sheet, visible page stack, short title block, signatures or route marks
- Hardware: two or three exposed metal rings
- Information: mission title, part number, date, short handwritten note
- Color: ivory + black + oxidized brown spots
- Best for: journeys, expeditions, plans, timelines, memory projects

## Paired Closed/Open Study

- State: paired-state-study
- Artifact: same binder shown closed above and open below, or side by side
- Layers: cover/index outside; pockets/forms/inserts inside
- Hardware: repeat the same clip, rings, tabs, and rivets across both states
- Information: one shared title and matching accession code
- Color: kraft/gray base + one consistent insert color
- Best for: systems, collections, publications, product narratives

## Compact Prompt Skeleton

```text
[Frame and black stage; artifact share and placement.]

[Artifact state, silhouette, camera angle, and subject interpretation.]

[Paper layers, forms, photos, diagrams, hardware, and wear.]

[Exact labels, numbering, stamps, handwriting, and information hierarchy.]

[Neutral palette, one accent family, metal finish, diffuse light, scan defects.]

[Hard avoids.]
```
