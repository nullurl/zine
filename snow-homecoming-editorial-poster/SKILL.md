# Snow Homecoming Editorial Poster

## Overview

Turn one reference image into a reusable editorial-poster grammar. Use it to generate new snow-night city homecoming covers without copying the original frame.

## Workflow

1. Read the reference image once and write a private grammar card.
2. Extract stable relationships, not exact coordinates.
3. Choose a new subject, city, title, and small copy.
4. Preserve at most three reference principles.
5. Change at least three of: camera angle, subject position, title scale, reading path, crop, architectural detail, or colour balance.
6. Generate the whole poster in one image pass, including the main title and hierarchy.

## Reference Grammar

Read [prompt-grammar.md](references/prompt-grammar.md) before composing. It contains the reusable visual rules extracted from the sample.

## Composition Rules

- Use a tall 3:4 poster.
- Use winter architecture as the emotional structure: an old city gate, wall, alley, courtyard, bridge, station entrance, or village street.
- Place one small human figure in motion, usually moving away or entering an opening.
- Let snow, wet stone, bicycle tracks, footprints, or slush carry the foreground texture.
- Reserve a calm text field, often in the upper-right or right third.
- Use restrained Song/Ming-style Chinese typography in ivory, pale sand, or warm off-white.
- Keep small copy sparse and meaningful. Use one short Chinese place/category line and one short English line only when helpful.
- Use warm practical light against cold blue-black dusk.
- Avoid red seals, ink splashes, pseudo-ancient ornaments, generic tourism composition, and landmark checklist logic.

## Reverse-Prompt Output

When the user asks to reverse-engineer a reference, provide:

- a short visual grammar card;
- a generation prompt;
- a negative prompt;
- exact recommended text fields;
- variation rules that prevent copying the reference.

Do not claim the reference is historical evidence unless provenance is provided.

## Generation Defaults

- Production mode: Mode B, complete image-generated poster layout.
- Text reserve: Medium, 25-38%.
- Editorial system: Night edition or city-memory document atmosphere.
- Title strategy: quiet publication title, not giant glyph construction.
- Size: vertical 3:4.
- Image style: cinematic documentary still, editorial cover, low saturation, weighted shadows.
