# Prompt Grammar

Use this skill for winter homecoming posters with a quiet editorial feel.

## Extracted Grammar Card

Reference hierarchy:
- Architecture is the dominant mass.
- Human figure is small but narratively central.
- Snow and wet stone create tactile evidence.
- Text sits in a calm dark field and does not fight the scene.

Type role:
- Main Chinese title is a readable editorial title, not calligraphy performance.
- Small Chinese metadata gives place and route mood.
- English line is short and literal.

Image role:
- The image behaves like a cinematic city-memory photograph.
- Warm lamp light creates the emotional center.
- Gate or opening gives the idea of return.

Geometry:
- Tall vertical frame.
- Large architectural wall creates quiet dark space.
- Path or alley pulls the eye inward.
- Foreground texture leads to the figure.

Palette:
- blue-black dusk
- warm amber lamp
- snow grey
- brick charcoal
- ivory title

## Stable Features

- tall 3:4 composition
- dark old-city wall, gate, alley, or courtyard
- snow, wet stone, or icy ground
- one small human figure moving away from camera
- warm practical light in a cold scene
- large calm text field with ivory or sand type
- low saturation, blue-black shadows, soft lamp glow

## What To Preserve

- winter atmosphere
- human scale against architecture
- snow or wet texture in the foreground
- restrained editorial typography
- feeling of return, delay, quiet travel home

## What To Change

- exact building silhouette
- exact figure route
- exact title wording
- exact type position
- exact snow density
- exact gate shape and camera height

## Prompt Skeleton

Create a vertical 3:4 Chinese editorial poster about snow, homecoming, and old-city atmosphere. Use a dark winter gate or alley, one small figure in motion, snow or wet ground, warm lamp light, muted blue-black shadows, and a large calm title area. Keep the mood quiet and cinematic, with restrained ivory typography and sparse metadata. Generate the full poster layout in one image pass. Do not copy the original composition.

## Example Prompt

Create a finished vertical 3:4 Chinese editorial poster, Mode B complete image-generated poster layout. Subject: snow falling over an old city gate at dusk, a small bundled figure pushing a bicycle toward a lit archway, wet stone road with footprints and tire marks, charcoal brick walls, old roofline partly cropped, warm lamp glow inside a cold blue-black winter atmosphere. Main title text, verbatim: "雪落下来，回家的路就慢了". Small line: "忻州 · 老城归途". English line: "THE WAY HOME." Typography: refined Song/Ming-style Chinese type, ivory colour, calm upper dark field, sparse metadata, no fake labels. Composition: large architectural mass, one human-scale narrative figure, clear text reserve, deep path perspective, foreground snow texture, quiet cinematic editorial cover. Palette: blue-black dusk, warm amber light, snow grey, brick charcoal, ivory title. Avoid: tourism poster, postcard, generic ancient China, red seal, ink splash, over-bright snow, excessive lanterns, crowded people, copied building silhouette, copied exact layout, QR code, watermark, pseudo-Chinese filler.

## Negative Prompt

Do not make a tourism advertisement, bright scenic postcard, festive red-and-gold holiday poster, generic guochao design, fantasy palace scene, landmark checklist, fake museum label, QR code, watermark, random small English, ink splash, red seal, decorative border, exaggerated snowstorm, or over-sharpened HDR image.
