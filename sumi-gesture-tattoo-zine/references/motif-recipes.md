# Motif Recipes

Choose one primary recipe. Add only one support vocabulary when the user asks
for a hybrid.

## Snake and Foliage

- Axis: long vertical S or wrapped double coil.
- Anchor: one readable head and two heavy body bends.
- Support: five to nine fragmented leaves or blade-like brush marks.
- Ink: dense head and lower coil, gray-scaled mid-body, white channels between.
- Avoid: photoreal reptile rendering, many heads, solid tribal fill.

## Bird Flight

- Axis: rising diagonal or vertical sequence.
- Anchor: one large bird with extended wing ribs.
- Echo: one or two smaller birds, each lighter than the anchor.
- Support: one moon wash or two small geometric medallions, optional.
- Motion: hairline arcs pass around wings rather than through every body.
- Avoid: flock scene, landscape, feathers rendered as perfect vectors.

## Butterfly Fracture

- Axis: vertical pair, diagonal ascent, or shoulder burst.
- Anchor: one butterfly with asymmetric ink-filled wing fragments.
- Echo: at most one smaller butterfly or moth shadow.
- Support: pale circular wash, orbit threads, two splatters.
- Avoid: decorative symmetry, colorful sticker art, botanical filler everywhere.

## Octopus Orbit

- Axis: compact head with three dominant tentacle loops.
- Anchor: head mass and one foreground tentacle with sparse sucker marks.
- Support: two ghost tentacles and a diluted circular wash.
- Motion: loops have different radii and taper into hairlines.
- Avoid: eight equally detailed tentacles, cartoon eyes, underwater scene.

## Lily Stem

- Axis: one thin rising stem with a diagonal branch.
- Anchor: one open flower and one closed bud.
- Support: three to six leaves rendered by single bristle pulls.
- Ink: open petals with black at joints, light gray ghost petal behind.
- Avoid: bouquet, vase, botanical plate labels, decorative border.

## Jellyfish or Smoke

- Axis: suspended cap or black node with one long falling current.
- Anchor: compact wash head and one decisive outer arc.
- Support: three to five separated filaments, one ghost wash.
- Accent: optional vermilion circle, cobalt cloud, or muted gold dust.
- Avoid: ocean scene, fantasy glow, soft airbrush-only marks.

## Hybrid Limit

For hybrids, pair one creature with one structural support only:

- snake + foliage;
- bird + moon/geometric node;
- butterfly + flower or orbit;
- octopus + wash circle;
- flower + one bird echo.

Do not combine snake, bird, butterfly, flower, moon, and geometry in one tattoo.
