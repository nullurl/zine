# Style Grammar

Use this file when reverse-engineering a reference, diagnosing style drift, or preserving the two-register photo/line system.

## Visual DNA

- Vertical poster, usually 3:4 or 4:5.
- Clean horizontal split near the middle.
- Upper panel is matte colored paper with visible grain.
- Lower panel is a full-width photograph, not a collage.
- Chinese poetic title and small date sit centered near the top.
- Thin off-white line drawing sits between title and split line.
- Upper line drawing abstracts the lower photo subject.
- Lower photo preserves natural light, local atmosphere, and subject specificity.

## Reference Extraction

Extract:

- split ratio and exact alignment
- upper paper hue and texture
- title length, date treatment, and text position
- line-art subject, density, line weight, and relation to photo
- lower photo subject, crop, lighting, and color temperature
- whether the image feels urban, garden, night, water, animal, object, or seasonal

## Line Art Rules

- Use pale ivory or off-white ink.
- Keep lines thin, slightly worn, and not perfectly vector-clean.
- Let the drawing fade subtly into the paper.
- Use contour, elevation, botanical, waterline, animal, or object-study drawing.
- Avoid heavy outlines, cartoon drawing, dense technical blueprint overload, or unrelated decoration.

## Photo Rules

- The lower image should be a single photo panel.
- Use quiet documentary or editorial photography, not product photography.
- The subject should connect directly to the line drawing above.
- Preserve natural color, but keep the palette restrained.

## Style Drift Corrections

If it becomes a postcard:

- remove frames, borders, stamps, stickers, and travel-card decoration
- specify clean horizontal split and full-width lower photograph

If it becomes a generic poster:

- strengthen matte paper texture, pale line drawing, small date, and split-panel relation

If it becomes too dense:

- reduce line drawing density and secondary text first
- keep upper panel as quiet negative space

If the photo dominates:

- enlarge the upper panel to about half the poster and strengthen the title/date/line drawing hierarchy
