# Prompt Recipes

Use these recipes when translating a theme into the split-panel system.

## Subject Trace

Use for one clear subject.

- upper: colored paper, title, date, thin traced drawing of the subject
- lower: full-width photo of the same subject
- good for statues, cats, flowers, fruit, fish, boats, street lamps

## Architectural Memory

Use for places and structures.

- upper: elevation-like line drawing of bridge, pavilion, tower, corridor, skyline, or temple
- lower: quiet real photo of the place
- colors: blue-gray, moss green, ochre, charcoal, or sunset orange

## Water And Animal

Use for lake, pond, river, fish, koi, duck, boat, rainwater, or floating objects.

- upper: pond-green or deep teal paper with waterline drawing
- lower: natural photo of water and animal/boat
- title should feel like a short observed moment

## Botanical Sky

Use for flowers, branches, spring, clear sky, garden, or trees.

- upper: soft sky blue, pale green, or warm kraft paper
- lower: daylight photo of flowers or branches
- line drawing should be airy and botanical

## Night Lamp

Use for snow, rain, street lamps, night roads, windows, or quiet city scenes.

- upper: dark brown-black or graphite paper
- lower: night photo with warm lamp glow
- line drawing may include rain/snow strokes

## Object Poem

Use for small objects, shop signs, drawings, souvenirs, cats, fruit, letters, or tabletop scenes.

- upper: warm kraft, ochre, muted orange, or blue-gray paper
- lower: real photo of the object situation
- line drawing can be playful but not cartoon-like

## Prompt Skeleton

```text
Portrait 3:4 split-panel editorial zine poster, clean horizontal split, upper [percentage] matte [paper color] paper panel and lower [percentage] full-width photographic panel, flat scanned print feeling, no border or mockup.

Upper panel: centered Chinese title "[TITLE]" near the top, small date "[DATE]" below, thin pale ivory line drawing of [line-art subject] occupying the middle of the paper panel, generous negative space, subtle paper grain, slightly worn ink.

Lower panel: quiet documentary photograph of [photo subject], [framing], [lighting], [palette], directly corresponding to the drawing above.

Restrained Chinese editorial typography, small date, pale linework, matte paper texture, mild print wear, controlled palette derived from [subject].

Avoid postcard frames, social templates, UI cards, scrapbook decoration, glossy 3D mockup, commercial ad hierarchy, logos, CTA, neon, cartoon, anime, unrelated line art, copied text, and long paragraphs.
```
