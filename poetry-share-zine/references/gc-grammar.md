# GC Minimal Zine Foundation for Poetry

## Semantic Gate

First build the image map in [semantic-routing.md](semantic-routing.md). The GC grammar controls material restraint and readability; it does not decide the subject. Never select a recipe from one keyword.

## Standard Identity

- Use a vertical paper poster: `4:5` for social delivery or `3:5` for the original poster proportion.
- Show a flat, orthographic printed surface with matte fibers, diffuse light, and no mockup shadow.
- Keep 55%-85% visually quiet paper or paper-like atmosphere according to the chosen style.
- Use one primary visual relation. Add no more than two supporting marks or fragments.
- Use one saturated ink, one controlled duotone, or a restrained two-ink opposition justified by the poem.
- Use xerox softness, risograph grain, letterpress bleed, halftone wear, scan noise, cyanotype exposure, photogram bloom, paper fibers, or slight misregistration according to the style family.
- Keep the emotional temperature literary and non-commercial, but let the poem determine whether it is tender, severe, playful, analytical, luminous, nocturnal, or surreal.

## Poetry Deviation Budget

- Allow the poem block to occupy roughly 15%-32% of the canvas.
- Allow one supplied keyword or repeated phrase to become a type-led anchor; do not enlarge several unrelated lines.
- Allow an image cluster around 8%-28%; named recipes may define a larger field when the poem needs a strong spatial relation.
- A landscape may appear as an edge field, map, portal, silhouette, or restrained scene fragment. It must preserve a high-contrast poem-safe region.
- Calligraphy is allowed only for one short supplied phrase. Typeset the complete poem deterministically.
- For a carousel, preserve one grid, typography system, and material process while letting the motif evolve card by card.

## Recipe Axes

Choose one item from each axis after semantic ranking.

- Layout: use a layout ID from [semantic-routing.md](semantic-routing.md) or a named recipe reference.
- Anchor: poem-derived object, species, weather trace, place fragment, diagram, material sample, shadow, gesture, or deterministic type structure.
- Typography: quiet column, spatial stanza field, phrase-to-anchor counterpoint, low-contrast echo, archive microline, or concrete-type constellation.
- Texture: xerox, risograph, letterpress, halftone, cyanotype, photogram, contact print, film grain, scan fibers, textile grain, or selected blur.
- Palette: choose from the poem's stated colors and temperature first; otherwise choose one hue whose material process supports the primary motif.

## Four-Paragraph Prompt Compiler

1. State ratio, surface, flat reproduction, quiet-space percentage, and text-safe region.
2. Name the primary motif, supporting motifs, action relation, placement, and scale.
3. Name style family, exact ink plan, material process, and sparse secondary marks.
4. State emotional temperature and hard avoids: no generated words, logo, watermark, unrelated person, unsupported sea/diver imagery, commercial hierarchy, glossy mockup, dense collage, or gratuitous colors.

## Thumbnail Gate

- The poem remains readable and the visual anchor remains recognizable.
- The primary motif is more specific than a generic emotional symbol.
- The palette survives thumbnail viewing without looking promotional.
- The composition differs structurally from recent variants.
- Reject stock photography, generic quote cards, decorative scrapbooks, and backgrounds that ignore the poem's image map.
