# Prompt Recipes

## Five-Paragraph Base Prompt

```text
Tall vertical [ratio] flat matte paper poster, [55%-88%] charcoal-black screenprinted field with subtle paper fibers and uneven ink density (toward 55%-65% for richer sea/dapple/woodcut display; optional dappled tree shadows 树影斑驳 breaking the field). One irregular [aperture family] opening sits [position], occupies [12%-35%], and leaves broad darkness above and beside it; no frame, mockup, gradient, or glossy depth.

The opening has a [tapered/arched/flame-like] silhouette edged by [branches/torn fibers/soot/chipped ink/emulsion peel], with only a few controlled detached flecks. It is physically legible as one opening, not a soft vignette or random grunge mask.

Inside the opening: [celestial sign], [distant layer], [middle layer], and [small foreground relationship]. For sea-heavy briefs add layered rolling waves with carved foam crests, a distant island or headland, a small boat or sailboat, wet-sand reflections, and shells or rocks. Render the scene as simplified screenprint or woodcut contour with realistic scale, 3-5 flat inks, anonymous silhouettes, and varied two-figure poses (one kneeling while the other stands, one leading, one with an arm raised).

Use [palette family] as the sole high-chroma anchor, isolated inside the aperture; keep the surrounding field and figures nearly black. Add halftone, paper grain, ink bleed, slight misregistration, eroded edges, and aged print softness; for woodcut enrichment add strong carved linework on waves, leaves, and rocks. No global color wash.

Optional tiny widely spaced title [exact short text] near the bottom margin. Avoid full-bleed sunset, stock vignette, random grunge, horror, gore, occult imagery, commercial movie poster, large title, credit block, photoreal faces, glossy CGI, neon, purple grading, teal-orange lighting, logos, signatures, watermarks, and copied source text.
```

## Shoreline Bond

Best for family, friendship, homecoming, care, and shared memory.

- aperture: canopy-window or cave-mouth
- sky: warm dusk with one small crescent
- distance: island or mountain ridge
- middle: calm water with horizontal contour marks; for richer sea briefs add layered rolling waves with carved foam crests, a small boat or sailboat, wet-sand reflections, and shells or rocks
- foreground: two or three figures with varied poses — one kneeling to touch the water while the other stands with an arm raised, or one leading and one following
- palette: ember coast

Keep the figures at the lower third of the aperture and below 8% of total page height. Vary pose and spacing so the pair reads as a living moment.

## Lone Return

Best for exile, return, solitude, and choosing a direction.

- aperture: hidden-door or eroded-ink
- interior: narrow road or path toward one small house light
- figure: one rear-facing silhouette, walking or standing
- palette: amber home or lemon passage
- optional title: one short place-name or verb

## Moon Over Water

Best for quiet, distance, waiting, grief, and night travel.

- aperture: flame-void or canopy-window
- celestial sign: one small crescent or dim disc
- distant ridge plus broad water band; add a small wooden boat, rocky headland, or moon-path reflections when the brief asks for more sea
- zero to two figures; a bench or empty pier is allowed; when two figures appear, vary poses (one sitting, one standing close; one walking into the water, one turning back)
- palette: moon ash with one warm moon-cream accent

## Field Passage

Best for growth, childhood, rural memory, and seasons.

- aperture: burned-paper or emulsion-tear
- layered field with one road, bicycle, animal, or walking pair
- palette: green ember
- use sparse linework and natural scale markers

## Object Memory

Best when the user supplies an object instead of a place.

- place one enlarged object silhouette inside the aperture
- embed a tiny horizon, moon, or road within the object
- keep the aperture and object as one readable cluster
- do not add unrelated people unless requested

## Regeneration Recipes

### Opening too large

Add: `the warm aperture occupies exactly about 20% of the page; at least 35% uninterrupted charcoal-black paper remains above it and wide dark margins remain on both sides`.

### Opening edge too soft

Add: `hard physical silhouette made from overlapping leaves, thin branches, chipped ink and torn fibers; no blur, glow, feathered mask, oval vignette, or gradient`.

### Interior too photographic

Add: `reduce the scene to four flat screenprint inks, simplified contour drawing, horizontal water marks, visible halftone and slight registration error; no photographic texture inside the aperture`.

### Figures too dominant

Add: `figures are tiny anonymous backlit silhouettes below the lower third, no faces, no costume detail, no dramatic pose`.

### Orange spreads into black field

Add: `warm ink exists only inside the opening and a tiny title; all surrounding paper stays neutral charcoal-black with no orange glow or global grading`.

### Richer sea and dappled shadows

Add: `layered rolling waves with carved white foam crests, a distant island or headland, a small boat or sailboat on the horizon, wet-sand reflections, and scattered shells or rocks; dappled tree shadows (树影斑驳) drift across the surrounding paper; the aperture widens to about 30%-35% of the page and the dark share drops to about 55%-65%`.

### More varied two-figure poses

Add: `two small silhouettes in expressive poses — one kneels to touch the water while the other stands behind with an arm raised toward the sky; use gesture and spacing, no faces`.

### Woodcut enrichment

Add: `strong carved linework on waves, leaves, rocks, and roof lines; woodcut grain replacing smooth fills; 4-5 flat inks with visible halftone and slightly rougher registration; carved leaf shapes and branch lines on the aperture edge; mottled ink and tree-shadow texture across the black field`.
