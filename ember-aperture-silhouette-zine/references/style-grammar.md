# Style Grammar

## Reference Deconstruction

The reference is a vertical poster dominated by charcoal-black printed texture. An irregular, tapering opening appears around the lower center. Dense foliage and branch silhouettes define the opening; the revealed scene contains a limited orange sunset, crescent moon, distant mountain, horizontal water marks, a pier, and three small human silhouettes.

The visual hierarchy is strict:

1. black printed field
2. warm irregular aperture
3. horizon and celestial sign
4. small relationship silhouette
5. tiny bottom typography

## Stable Features

### Black Field

- Occupies roughly 55%-88% of the page (default 75%-88%; reduce toward 55%-65% when the brief asks for richer sea, dappled shadows, or woodcut display).
- Reads as charcoal ink on matte paper, not a digital black fill.
- Contains subtle fibers, mottling, faded density, and a few controlled flecks.
- Keeps broad uninterrupted darkness above and beside the opening; optional dappled tree shadows (树影斑驳) may break the field when enrichment is requested.

### Aperture

- Irregular vertical arch or flame-like taper.
- Wider at the lower landscape and narrower toward the upper opening.
- Edges are made from foliage, branches, soot, fibers, chipped ink, or emulsion erosion.
- Occupies approximately 12%-35% of the page (widen toward 30%-35% when the scene carries more sea or woodcut detail).
- Includes only a few detached holes that echo the main opening.

### Interior Scene

- Uses simple layers: sky, celestial sign, cloud bands, distant land, water or field, foreground platform, silhouettes.
- Contains 3-5 printed colors rather than a full photographic spectrum.
- Uses contour lines and horizontal marks for water, clouds, terrain, and architecture.
- For sea-heavy briefs: layered rolling waves with carved foam crests, a distant island or headland, a small boat or sailboat, wet-sand reflections, and shells or rocks in the foreground.
- Figures remain small and backlit, with no face detail; vary poses when two figures appear (one kneeling, one standing; one leading, one following; one with an arm raised, one resting).

### Typography

- One tiny, widely tracked title at the bottom or side.
- Warm ink may echo the aperture color.
- No subtitle, credits, logo, or large headline by default.

## Minimal Zine Fusion

Keep the source Skill's one-anchor discipline, sparse type, print defects, vertical paper artifact, and quiet memory temperature. Reverse its tonal polarity: the negative space is dark printed paper, and the one color anchor is an opening rather than an object or cutout.

The aperture may be larger than the original Minimal Zine's 0.8%-2.5% accent because it carries the scene. Its chroma must still be isolated and singular.

## Palette Families

| Palette | Interior inks | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ember coast | peach, orange, tomato, mauve-gray | family, sea, return |
| amber home | amber, cream, rust, black | home, waiting, shelter |
| moon ash | pale cream, dusty rose, charcoal violet | night, memory, absence |
| lemon passage | lemon, vermilion, gray-purple | journey, signal, hope |
| green ember | pear green, amber, soot, muted cyan | field, forest, renewal |

Use one family per image. The surrounding field stays charcoal-black unless the brief asks for a lighter, richer composition, in which case the field may drop toward 55%-65% and the aperture may widen to about 30%-35%.

## Failure Diagnosis

### Aperture looks like a stock vignette

Replace soft blur with a physical edge system: branches, torn fibers, burned paper, chipped ink, or emulsion peel. Use an asymmetric tapered contour.

### Poster becomes a full sunset scene

Restate exact percentages and require uninterrupted black margins. Reduce the opening before simplifying the interior.

### Looks like horror

Remove threatening figures, red-black violence, deep cave realism, sharp claws, skull shapes, and ominous light. Make the interior relationship quiet and the darkness protective rather than hostile.

### Looks like a movie poster

Remove big title, dramatic backlight, lens flare, cinematic credits, photoreal faces, and spectacle. Restore tiny type, flat print inks, and anonymous silhouettes.

### Print texture becomes random grime

Limit distress to paper fibers, slight ink mottling, edge erosion, and a few detached flecks. Keep the central silhouette and page geometry clean.

## Enrichment Recipes

### Richer sea and dappled shadows

Add layered rolling waves with carved white foam crests, a distant island or headland, a small boat or sailboat on the horizon, wet-sand reflections, and scattered shells or rocks; let dappled tree shadows (树影斑驳) drift across the surrounding paper; widen the aperture to about 30%-35% and reduce the dark share to about 55%-65%.

### More varied two-figure poses

Vary the pair: one kneels or crouches while the other stands; one leads and the other follows; one raises an arm while the other rests; one walks into the water trailing a hand while the other turns back. Use gesture and spacing rather than faces.

### Woodcut enrichment

Add strong carved linework on waves, leaves, rocks, and roof lines; replace smooth fills with woodcut grain; use 4-5 flat inks with visible halftone and slightly rougher registration; carve leaf shapes and branch lines into the aperture edge; add mottled ink and tree-shadow texture across the black field.
