# Everyday Overlay Photo Zine Prompt Recipes

Use this file when selecting layout family, subject thread, or palette.

## Recipe Matrix

### window-cafe-board

Use for: cafe, window, rain, book, quiet drink, solo afternoon.

Prompt ingredients:

- large window or cafe table background
- green tea, latte, flower vase, open book, phone
- white outline boxes around tiles
- one circular music-player-like ornament only if useful

### room-fan-diary

Use for: fan objects, character merch mood, bedroom shelves, plush toys, collectable diary.

Prompt ingredients:

- blurred room or shelf background
- fictional illustrated cards, acrylic standees, plush toys, badges, phone case
- vertical soft text and small stars/arrows
- pink, blue, beige, and warm shadow accents

### garden-light-collage

Use for: forest, flowers, summer shade, outdoor cafe, green mood.

Prompt ingredients:

- deep green dappled background
- lilies, daisies, matcha drink, sunny tiles, shaded path
- high-contrast sunlight patches
- white handwritten notes and rectangular outlines

### tabletop-summer-board

Use for: dessert, soda, plush, tiled cafe, soft cute objects.

Prompt ingredients:

- tiled wall or cafe chair background
- plush toys, green soda, cake, daisies, small table
- pale blue/cream/mint palette
- star and cursor line marks

### transit-memory-board

Use for: bus, train, rain, umbrella, moving-window memories.

Prompt ingredients:

- dark transit interior background
- umbrella window, drink, flowers, notebook, small photo cards
- motion blur and reflected glass
- white lyric-like fragments, but not copied lyrics

### quiet-gray-room-board

Use for: concrete cafe, gray room, moody daylight, flowers and pastry.

Prompt ingredients:

- concrete wall, chair, table, window light
- cup, pastry, lily, cake plate, glass, book
- gray/wood/cream palette
- sparse white lines and tiny caption blocks

## Theme Translation

For a phrase:

- translate it into a believable daily memory scene and one short text fragment.

For a place:

- make that place the background scene and use tiles as close-up memories from the same visit.

For a flower:

- make one flower the primary object and repeat it in one or two tiles only.

For a drink or dessert:

- make cafe/tabletop objects the anchor and keep support tiles from the same outing.

For fan merch:

- use fictional character goods and avoid copying recognizable IP or exact reference art.

## Prompt Skeleton

```text
Horizontal [4:3 / 16:10 / 3:2] raster image, one softly blurred [background scene], muted [palette], personal lifestyle photo-collage mood, natural daylight or moody window light.

Layout: [recipe], with [5-12] overlapping rectangular photo tiles of varied size, arranged across the background with a loose diary rhythm, one primary tile larger than the rest, no rigid grid.

Subject thread: [primary object] plus supporting tiles showing [objects], all from the same believable day or room, soft photographic realism, mild blur and natural shadows.

Overlay system: thin white rectangular outline frames crossing over tile edges, small arrows, dotted icons, clover/star/tear/music-note marks, and gentle line geometry; keep overlays delicate, not UI.

Typography: short atmospheric text fragments "[TEXT]" in white or pale gray, small serif/cursive/vertical notes, no long copy, no copied identifiers, no real usernames or watermarks.

Texture and mood: soft scan grain, subtle vignette, muted color, diary-like romance; avoid commercial ads, app UI, strict grid, copied fan art, logos, neon, and clutter overload.
```
