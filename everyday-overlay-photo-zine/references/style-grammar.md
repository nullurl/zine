# Everyday Overlay Photo Zine Style Grammar

Use this file when preserving the reference look or correcting style drift.

## Reference Grammar

The target language is a lifestyle photo overlay collage:

- one full-canvas lifestyle photo background, often softly blurred or darkened
- several rectangular photo tiles laid over the background, with varied sizes and overlaps
- thin white outline rectangles floating across tile edges
- small white arrows, dotted clovers, stars, tear drops, music notes, or cursor-like marks
- soft poetic text fragments in white, sometimes vertical or cursive
- everyday subjects: cafe tables, green drinks, flowers, books, plush toys, figurines, phone, windows, chairs, desserts, gardens, buses, balconies
- muted natural palette with one day-specific color accent
- personal diary atmosphere, not a clean template

## Minimal Zine Carryover

Use these from Minimal Zine:

- restraint in attention route
- sparse poetic type
- gentle print/scan softness
- one coherent color identity
- memory-like mood

This skill is more photographic and more layered than Minimal Zine. The background can fill the frame; the zine discipline comes through controlled overlays and text.

## Collage Anatomy

Always specify:

- one background scene
- five to twelve photo tiles
- one primary object or tile
- thin white overlay geometry
- three to seven small line icons/marks
- one or two short text fragments

## Text Rules

- Use exact user text only when supplied as in-image copy.
- Otherwise invent short fictional fragments.
- Do not copy text from references.
- Do not render lyrics, long paragraphs, real handles, URLs, dates, or watermarks.

## Style Drift Corrections

If it becomes a UI:

- say `photographic lifestyle collage, not app interface, not dashboard`

If it becomes a rigid grid:

- say `overlapping irregular photo tiles, varied sizes, soft diary layout`

If it becomes too cluttered:

- reduce to one background scene, seven photo tiles, and five white line marks

If it loses the overlay style:

- add `thin white rectangular outline overlays crossing over photo edges`

If it copies fan art:

- replace with fictional illustrated merch, generic character goods, plush toys, or printed cards
