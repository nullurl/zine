# Prompt Recipes

## Holiday and Sunshine

- Background: blurred leafy street or café terrace
- Anchor: drink, meal, or seated travel moment
- Supports: window light, flowers, sidewalk, bag, sky, friend-free detail
- Overlays: white crop frames, one red arrow, sparse dots
- Type: handwritten title + small subtitle
- Grade: olive shadows and honey highlights

## Day-in-the-Life Board

- Background: blurred neighborhood or bedroom field
- Anchor: central afternoon activity
- Supports: breakfast, object in hand, transit, clothes, evening light
- Overlays: thin route line and 3-5 tiny labels
- Type: date and one short phrase
- Grade: soft-cyan or golden-afternoon

## Café and Street Memory

- Background: blurred storefront, trees, or rainy window
- Anchor: table scene or street corner
- Supports: cup, food, chair, flower, architecture, shadow, reflection
- Overlays: white rectangles, circle callouts, one short caption
- Type: script title with small editorial text
- Grade: warm olive and muted cream

## Botanical Interlude

- Background: blurred green park or courtyard
- Anchor: one flower or plant photo
- Supports: café, bench, window, sky, hand-held object, path
- Overlays: thin botanical callout boxes and small dot field
- Type: poetic title, nearly textless lower area
- Grade: soft green with one vermilion mark

## Evening Route

- Background: blurred streetlights, park edge, or bus window
- Anchor: sunset, road, or illuminated building
- Supports: meal, hands, ticket, reflection, flower, city detail
- Overlays: diagonal white route line and two arrows
- Type: short title + time marker
- Grade: dusk blue with one warm yellow accent

## Compact Prompt Skeleton

```text
[Landscape canvas, heavily blurred background, negative space, cluster scale.]

[Layout family, one memory thread, anchor photo, support windows, crop variation.]

[White borders, overlays, dots, arrows, connector lines, and order of overlap.]

[Short exact title, captions, type behavior, one accent hue, shared photo grade.]

[Film grain, soft-focus mood, personal album feeling, and hard avoids.]
```
