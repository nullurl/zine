# Cd Jewel Case Memory Zine

## Overview

Use this skill to turn an uploaded photo into a realistic Japanese indie CD album product mockup. The default output should feel like a physical acrylic CD case photographed on warm ivory paper: clean, quiet, soft, and finely detailed.

## Core Form

Treat the jewel case as the main product, not a decorative extra. Keep one transparent case, one half-visible disc, one full-cover photo, and a restrained spine title.

Use this structure for prompts:

1. Set the canvas to vertical 3:4 on warm ivory-white paper with large negative space.
2. Place a transparent plastic CD jewel case in the lower-middle area, lightly tilted.
3. Put the uploaded photo fully across the square cover, edge to edge, with no collage, no mat, and no white border.
4. Sample a soft low-saturation helper color from the photo for the left spine strip.
5. Put one centered thin English or Japanese title on the spine; keep all text minimal.
6. Show about half of a pearly silver-white CD on the right, with a small iridescent tint that echoes the photo.
7. Keep the lighting soft and realistic, with gentle plastic detail and no exaggerated reflection.

## Visual Rules

- Use warm ivory-white paper as the background; keep wide blank space above and around the case.
- Preserve the uploaded photo as the complete square cover image; do not crop away important content unless unavoidable.
- Use transparent clear plastic, faint scratches, real case edges, subtle latches, and soft cast shadows.
- Make the left spine a muted auxiliary color drawn from the photo, not a bright accent block.
- Use a thin, centered English or Japanese title on the spine; avoid large cover headlines unless the user asks.
- Make the CD pearly silver-white with a restrained photo-matching iridescent sheen.
- Keep the whole image like real independent album product photography: clean, soft, delicate, and believable.

## Prompt Strategy

When the user gives a photo, use it as the cover insert and preserve it as the dominant visual. Explicitly say the photo fills the square cover edge to edge, with no collage, no white border, no extra paper frame, and no added decorative inserts.

When the user gives only a theme, invent a single plausible square cover photo, but keep the album mockup rules unchanged.

When the user explicitly asks for a collage or memory-zine variant, allow a restrained collage inside the cover. Otherwise, do not add collage elements, torn paper, tape, stickers, handwritten notes, or white photo borders.

## Avoid

- full-bleed background scenes outside the CD case
- loud commercial album art
- big headline typography
- busy scrapbook layouts
- collage or white border around an uploaded photo unless explicitly requested
- fluorescent colors
- transparent colored acrylic
- exaggerated glossy reflections
- neon, chrome, or hard cinematic lighting
- complex multi-panel collages
