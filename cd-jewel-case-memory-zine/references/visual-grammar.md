# Visual Grammar

## Canonical Read

The style reads as a photographed Japanese indie CD album mockup on warm ivory-white paper. The case is slightly tilted in the lower-middle of a vertical 3:4 frame, the uploaded photo fills the square cover edge to edge, and about half of a pearly silver-white CD is visible on the right.

## Composition Pattern

- Use one jewel case as the main object.
- Use a vertical 3:4 frame.
- Place the case in the lower-middle area.
- Rotate it gently, usually only a few degrees.
- Let about half of the disc peek out from the right side.
- Keep generous warm ivory-white paper space above and around it.
- Use soft tabletop shadow, not hard studio shadow.

## Cover Image Pattern

- Put the uploaded photo inside the case as the full square cover.
- Fill the cover edge to edge with the photo.
- Preserve the photo's main subject and overall composition.
- Do not add collage, torn paper, tape, stickers, white borders, or paper mats by default.
- Only use collage if the user explicitly asks for a collage or zine variant.

## Text Pattern

- Put tiny vertical spine text on the left edge.
- Center a thin English or Japanese title on the spine.
- Keep cover text absent or extremely minimal unless the user provides exact text.
- Use light sans, thin serif, monospaced, or Japanese Mincho/Gothic-like type.
- Keep dates optional and small.

## Palette Pattern

- Base surface: warm ivory white paper.
- Spine strip: a soft, low-saturation auxiliary color sampled from the uploaded photo.
- Case: clear transparent plastic only.
- Disc: pearly silver-white with a restrained iridescent tint that echoes the photo.
- Avoid fluorescent colors, transparent colored acrylic, neon, and high-chroma accent blocks.

## Material Cues

- clear acrylic case
- full-photo printed insert
- pearly compact disc edge
- paper texture beneath
- gentle flash reflection
- slight surface scuffing
- subtle latches and hinge details

## Prompt Order

When drafting prompts, describe:

1. vertical 3:4 warm ivory paper background and negative space
2. jewel case lower-middle placement and slight tilt
3. uploaded photo fully filling the square cover with no collage or border
4. spine color sampled from the photo and centered thin title
5. half-visible pearly silver-white CD with restrained matching sheen
6. realistic soft product-photography lighting and material finish

## Useful Negative Constraints

- no flat poster layout
- no brand campaign look
- no glossy 3D render language
- no crowded collage
- no white border around the uploaded photo
- no dramatic shadows or neon
- no fluorescent color
- no transparent colored acrylic
- no exaggerated reflection
