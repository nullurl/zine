# Travel Ticket Memory Zine Prompt Recipes

Use this file when selecting a layout, palette, or prompt structure.

## Recipe Matrix

### single-memory-ticket

Use for: most single theme or photo requests.

Prompt ingredients:

- one large rounded card centered on a muted background
- top photo window, lower solid panel, bottom semicircle notch
- place title lower left, date/index below, barcode-like marks lower right
- soft shadow and matte card texture

### gallery-nine

Use for: multiple memories, city series, seasonal collections, or "make a set".

Prompt ingredients:

- 3x3 grid of color blocks
- one small ticket card centered in each block
- each card has a different photo subject and coordinated panel color
- repeated title system with small date/code variations

### photo-led-ticket

Use for: user-provided reference photos where the photo subject should remain recognizable.

Prompt ingredients:

- photo window occupies close to 70% of card
- lower panel is quiet and monochrome
- preserve photo subject, crop, and dominant palette
- metadata remains short

### seasonal-pass

Use for: month, season, travel diary, or festival prompts.

Prompt ingredients:

- one card with a month/date line
- palette tied to season: dusty blue blossom, rose fireworks, sage tree canopy, warm clay fish tank
- small fictional `NO.` index and barcode-like stripes

### full-bleed-card-study

Use for: close-up card object, strong material detail, or cover-like output.

Prompt ingredients:

- card fills most of canvas
- notch, shadow, and lower panel are prominent
- photo window large and immersive
- background remains a simple color field

## Theme Translation

For trees or green canopy:

- muted sage or deep olive background, leafy photo window, title `TREE LIGHT`, date `2026 - 06`, olive barcode marks.

For fish tank:

- warm clay or beige background, goldfish photo window, title `FISH WINDOW`, date `2026 - 08`, white barcode marks.

For fireworks:

- rose red or smoke brown background, night spark photo window, title `NIGHT SPARK`, date `2026 - 08`, white barcode marks.

For old roof or gray street:

- smoke gray background, roof tile photo window, title `OLD ROOF`, date `2026 - 02`, white type and barcode marks.

For blossoms and glass building:

- dusty blue background, pale blossom photo window, title `SPRING CITY`, date `2026 - 03`, navy type and barcode marks.

## Prompt Skeleton

```text
Portrait [3:4 / 4:5 / 3:5] raster image with a [palette] full-page matte background, one [single centered / 3x3 grid] rounded travel-memory ticket card system, soft shallow shadow, paper/card texture, calm minimal-zine negative space.

Each card has rounded corners, a top photo window occupying [60%-70%] of the card, a solid lower information panel occupying [30%-40%], a visible semicircle notch cut from the bottom center edge, tiny seam ticks between photo and panel, and barcode-like decorative stripes in the lower right.

Photo subject: [subject], [crop and mood], [preserve reference palette/shape if supplied], everyday memory-photo look, not commercial travel photography.

Typography: bold compact uppercase title "[TITLE]" in lower left, small date/index "[DATE/NO.]" beneath, short fake non-identifying code near the barcode, clean sans, sparse text only, no copied reference text.

Matte printed card, subtle scan noise, low-to-medium contrast, soft shadow; avoid real brands, real barcodes or QR codes, CTA, phone UI, transportation ticket literalism, glossy mockup, dense scrapbook, long text, copied identifiers, and watermarks.
```
