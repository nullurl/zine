# GC Minimal Zine Foundation for Book Excerpts

## Semantic Gate

First build the evidence map in [semantic-routing.md](semantic-routing.md). The GC grammar controls tactile restraint, reading comfort, and print identity; it does not decide the subject or force a named recipe.

## Standard Identity

- Use a vertical paper poster: `4:5` for social delivery or `3:5` for the original poster proportion.
- Show a flat, orthographic printed surface with matte fibers, diffuse light, and no mockup shadow.
- Keep 50%-80% visually quiet paper or paper-like space according to excerpt length and style.
- Use one primary source-grounded relation and no more than two supporting marks or fragments.
- Use one saturated ink, one controlled duotone, or a restrained two-ink opposition justified by the passage.
- Use xerox, risograph, letterpress, halftone, cyanotype, photogram, contact print, film grain, textile grain, scan fibers, or slight misregistration according to the selected style.
- Keep the result editorial and reflective rather than commercial, while allowing scientific, urban, domestic, historical, playful, severe, or luminous tones when supported.

## Excerpt Deviation Budget

- Allow the reading block to occupy about 20%-42% of the canvas.
- Keep title, author, translator, and source details smaller than the excerpt.
- Allow an image cluster around 8%-28%; named recipes may use a larger field when the passage requires a strong spatial relation.
- Highlight one supplied phrase or use one source-related anchor; do not oversize both.
- Use open-book imagery only when a supplied page, physical reading act, or confirmed book artifact is materially important.
- Use gathered marginalia only when multiple source artifacts are supplied.
- For a carousel, preserve one grid, typography system, material process, and source hierarchy.

## Recipe Axes

Choose after semantic evidence ranking.

- Layout: use a layout ID from [semantic-routing.md](semantic-routing.md) or a named recipe reference.
- Anchor: source-grounded object, species, weather trace, architecture, diagram, material sample, page fragment, shadow, gesture, or verified artifact.
- Typography: calm excerpt column, phrase-to-anchor counterpoint, archive source line, low-contrast echo, strict analytical grid, or spacious literary block.
- Texture: xerox, risograph, letterpress, halftone, cyanotype, photogram, contact print, film grain, textile grain, scan fibers, or selected blur.
- Palette: use colors or temperature present in the passage first; otherwise choose one hue whose process supports the primary subject.

## Four-Paragraph Prompt Compiler

1. State ratio, printed surface, flat reproduction, quiet-space percentage, and reading-safe region.
2. Name the primary motif, supporting motifs, action/argument relation, placement, and scale.
3. Name style family, exact ink plan, material process, and restrained source marks.
4. State mood and hard avoids: no generated words, logo, watermark, fabricated cover or metadata, unrelated person, unsupported sea/diver imagery, commercial hierarchy, glossy mockup, dense collage, or gratuitous colors.

## Thumbnail Gate

- Reading comfort dominates and the anchor remains recognizable.
- Every motif is grounded in the passage or verified source material.
- The palette survives thumbnail viewing without resembling an advertisement.
- The source-safe region stays quiet and high contrast.
- Reject fake covers, generic quote cards, stock campaigns, decorative scrapbooks, and backgrounds that ignore the evidence map.
