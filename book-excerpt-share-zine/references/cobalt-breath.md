# Cobalt Breath Excerpt Recipe

Use this optional named recipe only when water, breath, or depth is the top-ranked relation in the passage evidence map, when a supplied visual reference clearly uses this identity, or when the user explicitly requests it. A matching keyword alone is insufficient.

## Canonical Layout IDs

Choose exactly one ID and reproduce its geometry. Do not invent or rename a layout.

- `upper-text-lower-sea-strip`: passage in the upper third; one low ragged cobalt sea strip in the middle or lower-middle.
- `upper-text-lower-organic-pool`: passage upper-left or upper-center; one oval, leaf-like, or brush-masked pool below.
- `left-specimen-right-passage`: one small cobalt person/object specimen at left-middle; passage in a calm right column.
- `left-passage-tall-water-window`: short passage on the left; one tall torn cobalt bubble/depth window on the right.
- `upper-passage-lower-letter-sea`: passage upper-left; one wide lower cobalt field with a letter, page, rope, or hand partly submerged.
- `source-fragment-depth-axis`: one tiny source fragment near the top; one narrow diver/body vignette on a long depth axis below.
- `split-metaphor-relation`: passage on the left; one person-object relation inside a shared organic cobalt field on the right.

Forbidden substitutes: `ripple-title`, `centered-strip`, open-book display, hanging rice-paper strip, scroll, wall gallery, framed rectangular illustration, ornate English title, calligraphy poster, or red seal.

## Structure

- Canvas: vertical `3:4` preferred, `4:5` allowed for feed delivery.
- Paper: warm ivory/oatmeal with visible fibers, specks, scratches, and flat scan softness.
- Quiet field: 55%-75%.
- Passage block: 4-8 lines or one compact paragraph, about 12%-24%.
- Vignette: one 18%-35% cobalt duotone image with brush, torn, pool, strip, tall-window, specimen, or split-relation boundary.
- Source: exact book/author line below the passage; translation or reader note must be labeled and separated.

## Layout Choices

- upper passage / lower sea strip
- upper passage / lower organic pool
- left specimen / right passage
- left passage / tall water window
- upper passage / lower submerged letter
- small source fragment / vertical depth axis
- left passage / right conceptual relation

## Metaphor Choices

- breath and bubbles
- fear and depth
- courage and an opened letter or first movement
- growth and an unstable sand structure
- writing and a paper trail meeting the sea
- rescue and a small life changing one world
- body and a scale ignored by the sea
- memory and a submerged object

Choose one relation and tie it to the supplied passage; do not add generic ocean imagery merely for atmosphere.

Before choosing a person or diver, consider passage-specific nonhuman anchors such as a tide chart, shell, wet page, harbor light, air pocket, rope, reflection, salt trace, or horizon notch. Use a body only when the passage makes it primary.

## Background Prompt

Use the normal four-paragraph compiler, but name and implement the selected canonical layout geometry, warm fibrous paper, one metaphor relation, and cobalt/gray-blue halftone reproduction. Require no text, no second hue, no clean photo rectangle, no open-book or hanging-scroll fallback, no red seal, no ornate title, no fabricated cover, no full-bleed scene, no glossy depth, and no commercial hierarchy.

Typeset exact passage, source, and any verified translation afterward.
