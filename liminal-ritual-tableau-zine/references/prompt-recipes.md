# Prompt Recipes

Use these as structural recipes. Replace all bracketed variables and avoid copying sample wording mechanically.

## Five-Paragraph Prompt Shape

```text
[Format, photographic field, quiet-space share, layout family, anchor placement, figure scale, horizon.]

[Subject, garment/material, pose, camera distance, one precise action, expression or anonymity.]

[Threshold object, physical relation to the subject, one transformation, practical staging and believable material behavior.]

[Palette mode, exact accent event and visual share, light source, lens/capture character, grain and matte reproduction.]

[Optional exact short text and placement. Hard avoids: generic fantasy, fashion advertising, horror, ornate religion, glossy CGI, dense collage, malformed anatomy, watermarks, signatures, long fake text.]
```

## Single Portal

Best for passage, identity, waiting, memory, exile, and return.

- One life-size freestanding rectangle or narrow door.
- Figure stands behind, beside, or halfway through it.
- Portal contains one different state: smoke, pale sea, fire, blackness, or moving cloth.
- Keep the environment nearly empty and the geometry orthogonal.

Prompt seed:

```text
Vertical 2:3 medium-format editorial photograph, [field], 60% quiet space, single-portal composition. A solitary [figure] in [fabric] stands [pose] at a freestanding [threshold]. Inside the opening, [element] behaves as if it were a real material installation; it touches the garment at one clear seam of transformation. [palette], [light], matte fine-grain print, sparse [title] aligned to the lower frame edge.
```

## Ritual Triptych

Best for becoming, loss, healing, weather, and temporal change.

- Panel 1: approach or intact state.
- Panel 2: contact with the element.
- Panel 3: transformed or absent state.
- Lock camera height, figure scale, palette, and background.
- Vary one thing only: smoke volume, water depth, fabric spread, fire stage, or reflection.

Do not create three unrelated poster ideas.

## Reflection Split

Best for doubles, memory, self-recognition, dream, grief, and hidden life.

- Use a crisp waterline, horizontal mirror, wet glass, or black reflective floor.
- Above and below share perspective but disagree in one symbolic feature.
- Keep ripple distortion local and physically plausible.
- Ensure the reflected anatomy aligns before it transforms.

## Specimen Grid

Best for study, evidence, identity fragments, body-language systems, and material research.

- Four, six, or nine cells with exact gutters.
- Repeat one subject under one capture condition.
- Each cell changes one variable.
- Add tiny sequence numbers only; no caption blocks.
- One cell may carry the chromatic event.

## Stacked Altar

Best for suspension, burden, ancestry, ascent, and ritual installation.

- Build a vertical axis of two or three elements.
- Maintain visible attachment, support, or suspension logic.
- Leave wide lateral silence.
- Use gallery or fog-field lighting so shadows explain scale.

## Eclipse Landscape

Best for distance, fate, pause, industrial memory, and cosmic time.

- Small figure at lower third.
- One black or dim disc occupies the upper field.
- Use severe horizon, sparse architecture, salt plain, or concrete ruin.
- The accent is only an amber rim, rust pool, or tiny fire.

## Framed Apparition

Best when the human figure is absent or secondary.

- A freestanding frame is the main object.
- Smoke, fabric, branches, flame, water, or an impossible weather state appears inside.
- Make the frame's surface, weight, shadow, and contact with ground believable.
- One trace of human presence is enough: footprints, empty garment, hand, or reflection.

## Regeneration Recipes

### Too glossy or synthetic

Add: `hand-built practical set, imperfect fabric edges, physically supported frame, broad overcast light, matte fiber print, subtle film grain, natural skin texture`.

Remove: `cinematic masterpiece, epic, ethereal beauty, ultra-detailed, glowing aura, luxury editorial`.

### Too busy

Set exact limits: `one figure, one frame, one material event, no supporting props, 65% quiet field, no border or decorative collage`.

### Transformation is unclear

State the contact point and transition: `the lower hem crosses the waterline and continues as a dark smoke plume with matched width and believable reflection`.

### Accent color spreads everywhere

State: `all surfaces remain neutral; rust red appears only in the reflected pool and occupies about 5% of the frame; no global color grading`.

### Anatomy or hands fail

Use a rear-facing or distant full figure, simplify the hand action, and state `two anatomically correct hands, fingers naturally grouped, no duplicate limbs` only when hands are visible.

### Reference match is superficial

Extract and state five concrete controls: quiet-space percentage, figure scale, threshold geometry, material contrast, and light direction. Ignore source-specific costume, text, logo, and signature.
