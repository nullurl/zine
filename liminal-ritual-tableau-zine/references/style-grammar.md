# Style Grammar

## Core Identity

The style is a restrained surreal photograph staged as a private ritual. It should feel found between an art-school performance document, a quiet fashion-independent editorial, a gallery installation record, and an archival zine plate. The image is uncanny because one physical law changes, not because the frame is filled with fantasy detail.

## Stable Features

### Field

- Vertical 2:3 by default; 3:2 landscape only when the environment is essential.
- Pale, low-information fields: fog, overcast sky, concrete, empty gallery wall, salt flat, black water, dark soil.
- 45%-70% visual quiet. Empty space is active pressure around the ritual event.
- Edges stay calm; the central event is not crowded by decorative framing.

### Figure

- One solitary human figure is the default.
- White, bone, gray, or weathered fabric creates a soft mass against a severe field.
- Useful poses: frontal stillness, back turned, kneeling, waist-deep in water, profile at a threshold, suspended without theatrical motion.
- Hands are simple and visible only when they serve an action: holding a frame, touching water, carrying a branch, covering the face.
- The person is not a fantasy hero. Wardrobe should look handmade, distressed, ceremonial, and materially possible.

### Threshold Objects

- A rectangular frame, doorway, arch, mirror, pane, waterline, aperture, or eclipse disc gives the event geometric clarity.
- The threshold should contact the figure or landscape: held, crossed, reflected, submerged, burned, or filled.
- Prefer one strong shape to many symbolic ornaments.

### Material Transformation

The most important effect is a visible change of state:

- cloth becomes cloud, wing, wave, plume, or empty skin
- smoke erases the head or exits a frame
- a dress continues below water as a different body
- flame occupies an empty portal
- a reflection contradicts the standing figure
- broken glass splits one face into two temporal states
- rain on glass duplicates or dissolves a portrait

Transformations must have contact points, gravity, scale, and believable light. Describe practical staging, compositing, projection, reflection, or long exposure rather than abstract magic.

### Sequence

- Triptychs show approach, contact, transformation.
- Stacked scenes use one vertical axis and shared horizon logic.
- Specimen grids repeat one variable: face angle, fabric behavior, hand gesture, smoke stage, or reflective distortion.
- Keep spacing exact and backgrounds consistent. A grid is evidence, not decoration.

## Color Logic

Neutrals occupy 88%-98% of the image. The accent is an event, not a palette wash.

| Mode | Dominant field | Accent event | Best use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| bone-fog | bone, pearl, fog gray | none or charcoal | memory, waiting, silence |
| ash-fire | ash, white, soot | ember orange | sacrifice, change, signal |
| silver-smoke | silver, graphite, blue-gray | cold metallic glint | body, reflection, estrangement |
| black-water-red | black water, white cloth | rust or blood-orange without gore | grief, return, warning |
| eclipse-amber | concrete, smoke, black | amber rim | time, distance, cosmic pause |

Avoid purple grading, cyan shadows with orange skin, and uniform beige nostalgia.

## Light and Capture

- Overcast daylight, flat winter sun, diffused gallery light, wet ambient reflections, or localized firelight.
- Medium-format photographic restraint: stable perspective, moderate focal length, fine grain, broad tonal transitions.
- Matte print and slight paper tooth can be visible, but the result should remain a photograph rather than a paper collage.
- Use shallow depth of field only for wet-glass portraits. Environmental tableaux need readable spatial relations.

## Typography

- Optional, small, and editorial.
- One title of 2-6 words; one small sequence number, date, or coordinate line.
- Narrow serif, restrained grotesk, monospaced archive type, or typewriter text.
- Type may align to a frame edge or panel gutter, but never cover the face or transformation.

## Failure Diagnosis

### Looks like fantasy key art

Reduce costume detail, dramatic pose, weapons, backlight, and environmental spectacle. Replace magical language with handmade props, practical smoke, water reflection, and overcast photography.

### Looks like a fashion advertisement

Remove brand hierarchy, perfect styling, beauty retouching, luxury surfaces, and seductive pose. Add stillness, distressed material, imperfect props, and unresolved narrative.

### Looks like horror

Remove wounds, threatening darkness, contortion, demonic symbols, and aggressive red. Keep the uncanny event quiet and nonviolent.

### Looks like random surreal collage

Return to one thesis, one threshold, and one transformation. Make every secondary symbol physically touch or explain the primary event.

### Feels visually empty rather than tense

Increase scale contrast, clarify the portal geometry, strengthen one dark-light boundary, or give the figure one legible action. Do not fill the space with more objects.
