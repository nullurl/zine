# Green Reverie Collage Zine Prompt Recipes

Use this file when selecting a layout, translating a theme, or creating variations.

## Recipe Matrix

### pond-stack

Use for: pond, river, searching, koi, watery memory, reflective mood.

Ingredients:

- water reflection base
- central tree or pond photo rectangle
- small koi/water strip
- one botanical close-up
- two or three clipped caption strips
- dotted paper border or small cream stars

### adventure-panel

Use for: life adventure, walkway, childhood, travel memory, garden path.

Ingredients:

- upper textured green paper field
- lower blurred garden path photo
- anonymous pale silhouette or simple figure
- tiny line drawing or star mark
- small serif title

### torn-road-dream

Use for: city nature, road, tree canopy, going somewhere, summer route.

Ingredients:

- large tree photo on one side
- torn road window through the center
- sky or cloud paper insert
- one small cutout icon
- no visible brands or street names

### lotus-reverie

Use for: lotus, meditation, dream, mountain, moon, reading, quiet surrealism.

Ingredients:

- soft landscape field
- single tall lotus or flower
- anonymous seated silhouette or stone/hand symbol
- tiny moon or timestamp-like mark
- muted pink and green palette

### star-pond-archive

Use for: pond archive, lotus leaves, old film, memory collection.

Ingredients:

- grayscale lotus or plant photo as upper field
- green textured paper lower field
- central pond photo rectangle
- cream star scatter
- one small monochrome inset

### quote-water-page

Use for: poetic sentence, searching, lost-and-found mood.

Ingredients:

- watery reflection base
- stacked forest/flower/fish photo fragments
- rounded caption strips
- dot border or polka-dot paper block
- no copied quotes unless the user supplies exact copy

### sky-paper-memory

Use for: summer, sky, simple joy, music-like memory.

Ingredients:

- blue sky or blue paper base
- one silhouetted flower or tree
- small paper photo card
- cream dots, stars, or music marks
- short title only

## Theme Translation

For "searching":

- pond-stack, moving water, tree canopy, small fish strip, title "A Soft Search"

For "summer":

- sky-paper-memory, pale blue sky, green leaf cutout, cream star dots, title "Summer Drift"

For "garden path":

- adventure-panel, blurred walkway, mint silhouette, tiny star drawing, title "Life Adventure"

For "lotus":

- lotus-reverie, tall lotus, soft pond reflection, anonymous figure or moon symbol, title "Lotus Dream"

For "city trees":

- torn-road-dream, tree canopy and road window, small cloud paper, no logos, title "Green Route"

For "pond memory":

- star-pond-archive, lotus leaves, cream stars, green texture field, small monochrome inset, title "Pond Archive"

## Prompt Skeleton

Use this structure and replace bracketed fields:

```text
Portrait [2:3 or 3:5] green dream-collage zine on [base field], [negative-space share] quiet field, soft daylight, scan softness, paper grain, no mockup.

[Layout family] with [collage zone size/location], layer order: [base], [main photo fragment], [supporting fragments], [paper fields], [surreal insert], [marks].

Photo fragments: [specific water/forest/road/lotus/sky subjects], [crop formats], [torn or soft edges], [overlap behavior], [gentle scale shift].

Typography: title "[SHORT TITLE]", [caption strip or date mark], [serif/typewriter/script voice], original short text only, no copied reference captions, no logos.

Palette and texture: [green palette], [counter-accent], watery blur, dappled light, faded paper fibers, dot pattern, tiny stars, print wear, slight misregistration.

Mood and constraints: [emotional temperature]; avoid seamless landscape, clean UI, social template, polished scrapbook, brand marks, media screenshots, identifiable people, long text, and dense sticker clutter.
```
