# Green Reverie Collage Zine Style Grammar

Use this file when a request asks to reverse-engineer references, merge visual grammars, or correct style drift.

## Reverse-Engineered Reference Grammar

The reference set shares this visual language:

- green and water dominate: pond reflections, tree canopies, lotus leaves, forest paths, grass, and soft sky
- photographs are layered as rectangles, torn windows, photo strips, soft-focus panels, and partial cutouts
- paper textures are visible: pale green grain, dotted cloth-like fields, white margins with small green dots, rough blue paper
- gentle surrealism: oversized flower, anonymous silhouette, road inside tree collage, floating cloud paper, symbolic hand or moon, tiny figure or miniature icon
- small decorative marks: cream stars, dots, simple line drawing, timestamp-like numerals, soft paper labels
- text is clipped and short: title, one caption strip, or a few lines; it functions as paper object
- mood is dreamy and reflective, not high-drama fantasy

Do not copy exact captions, logos, media frames, or recognizable people. Extract only composition and material behavior.

## Minimal Zine Fusion

Bring from Minimal Zine:

- controlled negative space
- one main attention route
- short type
- single restrained color logic
- paper fibers, scan wear, and imperfect alignment
- memory-like editorial mood

Modify for green reverie:

- allow larger layered photo zones than Minimal Zine
- use green photo fields and textured paper as the base
- allow soft surreal inserts and multiple photo fragments
- keep clutter lower than scrapbook: every fragment must support the same memory route

## Material And Mark Vocabulary

Use concrete visual elements:

- base fields: watery reflection photo, pale green textured paper, polka-dot green fabric, soft sky paper, grayscale botanical photo
- photo fragments: rectangular forest crop, pond strip, lotus detail, road window, tree canopy, koi/water movement, flower close-up
- cut edges: torn white border, soft feathered photo edge, rounded caption strip, taped rectangle, imperfect overlap
- surreal inserts: anonymous pale silhouette, small boat drawing, paper cloud, moon on a hand, lotus scale shift, tiny timestamp, small bird-like cutout, symbolic eye crop
- marks: cream stars, green dots, dotted border, tiny plus signs, simple line drawing, faded date code
- type: serif title, typewriter strip, translucent caption, tiny stamped phrase

Avoid generic "dream collage" without naming the layer types.

## Composition Ratios

Default portrait:

- base paper/photo field: 45%-70%
- layered collage zone: 30%-60%
- text: under 8%
- marks: 1%-8%
- surreal insert: one main insert or two tiny inserts

Reference-like dense collage can use 60%-75% image coverage, but should keep a border, soft field, or quiet top/bottom area.

## Light And Texture

Use:

- soft daylight, watery glare, and dappled shade
- motion blur on water or walking path
- faded green paper grain
- old print softness and scan noise
- shallow composited depth, not heavy 3D shadows

Avoid:

- polished travel-photo realism
- hard-edged digital mood-board grid
- luxury poster lighting
- dark cyberpunk or neon botanical annotation
- too-clean scrapbook stationery

## Style Drift Corrections

If the output becomes a single landscape:

- add "visible layered photo rectangles, torn paper windows, caption strips, and dotted paper border"

If it becomes a clean scrapbook:

- remove washi-tape/sticker overload
- add "soft surreal scale shift, watery blur, green paper grain, imperfect photo cutouts"

If it becomes too busy:

- choose one layout family and remove half the fragments
- keep one main memory field and two supporting inserts

If text is copied or too long:

- replace with one original short title and one clipped caption strip

If it becomes too green and flat:

- add cream paper, sky blue, lotus pink, koi-orange, or black type as a controlled counter-accent
