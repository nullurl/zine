# Art Trash Journal Zine Style Grammar

Use this file when a request asks to reverse-engineer a reference, merge visual grammars, or correct style drift.

## Reverse-Engineered Reference Grammar

The supplied reference reads as an art-trash journal object:

- old newspaper pages or book pages as the outer base, with visible tan columns and aged ink
- a green painted board or paper sheet laid on top, slightly crooked
- dense miniature landscape made from moss, fiber, dried flowers, plastic beads, faux pearls, tiny branches, toy-like ladder, small cage, tiny bottle, and paper scraps
- heavy handmade relief: glue shine, raised objects, uneven edge, inconsistent scale, imperfect placement
- saturated green as the main material field, with cyan-blue berry clusters, white pearl lines, clear gems, red tiny flowers, and yellow paper flecks
- rectangular or book-spread structure, photographed from above with shallow shadows
- mood: cheap discarded materials turned into a private magical page

Do not copy the exact composition or visible text. Extract only the material grammar.

## Minimal Zine Fusion

Bring from Minimal Zine:

- aged paper as a main visual field
- restrained typography
- strong compositional breathing room
- one concentrated attention structure
- scan noise, old-print wear, matte paper
- a clear color anchor visible at thumbnail scale

Modify for art-trash:

- the attention structure can be thicker, messier, and more tactile than Minimal Zine
- negative space can be 45%-75% rather than 70%-90%
- color can occupy 10%-35% when it is inside one controlled collage island
- allow cheap craft materials, but keep a hierarchy: base paper, dense anchor, small debris trail

## Material Vocabulary

Use specific reclaimed materials:

- base: old newspaper, book page, ledger paper, cardboard, kraft paper, stained receipt, torn envelope
- paint: cracked acrylic, sponge-painted card, uneven green wash, dry-brushed pigment, scratched ink
- plant-trash: moss fiber, dried petals, twigs, seed pods, faux leaves, pressed weeds
- cheap shine: plastic rhinestones, clear cabochons, faux pearls, sequins, glossy glue, tape glare
- object scraps: toy ladder, tiny bottle, wire cage, paper ticket, torn wrapper, old label, matchbox, staple, bent tag
- fibers: gauze, thread, embroidery floss, frayed yarn, mesh, lace scrap
- damage: curled corner, ripped edge, smudged ink, glue puddle, dust, wrinkled paper, bent cardboard

Avoid generic "mixed media collage" without material names.

## Composition Ratios

Default:

- canvas: portrait 2:3 or 3:5
- aged paper base: 50%-75%
- dense collage island: 20%-45%
- debris trail: 3%-12%
- typography: under 5%
- accent color: 1%-6% outside the dominant material color

For a reference-like lush page, the collage island can reach 45%-60%, but still keep a readable border or open field.

## Light And Camera

Use:

- overhead flat-lay or scanned-object camera
- diffuse daylight
- shallow contact shadows around raised scraps
- visible paper fibers and print wear
- small specular highlights only on glue, beads, rhinestones, or plastic

Avoid:

- cinematic forest lighting
- deep perspective diorama
- macro depth-of-field blur
- glossy product-photo perfection

## Style Drift Corrections

If the image becomes a clean scrapbook:

- add "cheap plastic beads, glue puddles, torn wrappers, moss fiber, bent cardboard, awkward handmade scale"
- remove "elegant stationery, neat stickers, polished washi tape"

If the image becomes a fantasy landscape:

- add "visible old newspaper base, raised paper scraps, top-down handmade collage object"
- remove "immersive forest, realistic scene, cinematic depth"

If the image becomes uncontrolled clutter:

- restrict to one dense island and one debris trail
- increase visible blank paper to at least 55%

If the output is too cute:

- use acidic green, tan newspaper, black ink, scratched acrylic, tarnished staples, and torn labels
- avoid pastel stickers and cartoon characters

If the output loses material depth:

- specify 1-5 mm relief, lifted corners, glue shine, bead highlights, curled scraps, and shallow shadows
