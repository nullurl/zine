# Art Trash Journal Zine Prompt Recipes

Use this file when selecting a layout, translating an abstract theme, or creating variations.

## Recipe Matrix

### trash-island

Use for: default art-trash journal images and reference fusion.

Prompt ingredients:

- aged newspaper or book-page base
- one irregular painted card or torn board floating in the page
- dense reclaimed-material island
- one short title partly printed or stamped near the island
- dominant color plus one small accent

### altered-book-window

Use for: bookish, memory, secret place, miniature scene, old story themes.

Prompt ingredients:

- open old book or newspaper spread
- torn window or painted rectangle
- miniature scene built from scraps inside the window
- small paper tabs and half-buried text fragments

### junk-altar

Use for: object, devotion, music, grief, collection, ritual, or memory themes.

Prompt ingredients:

- compact shrine-like pile of low-value objects
- paper label, thread, dried petals, beads, foil, and one central object
- strong blank paper around the altar
- tiny title like a field note

### corner-spill

Use for: rain, street, travel, diary, casual memory, or loose hand-page themes.

Prompt ingredients:

- debris enters from one corner
- open aged paper field remains dominant
- strings, scraps, tiny beads, ticket bits, glue marks
- one color thread guides the reading path

### specimen-trash

Use for: one object, found item, shell, flower, leaf, key, wrapper, or relic.

Prompt ingredients:

- one reclaimed object treated like a specimen
- small label and index mark
- surrounding trash particles are minimal
- high negative space and old-print texture

### moss-diorama-page

Use for: garden, forest, tropical, fairy-tale-adjacent, green memory, or magic-trash themes.

Prompt ingredients:

- old newspaper base
- green painted card or torn board
- moss fiber, twigs, dried flowers, faux pearls, clear beads, tiny bottle, toy-like ladder or cage
- keep the camera overhead and make the scene visibly handmade

### type-and-debris

Use for: posters, poems, slogans, punk, article ideas, or language-led themes.

Prompt ingredients:

- torn type and rough letterpress as the main anchor
- debris embedded in letters or around the type
- one vivid ink color and scattered trash marks
- no clean commercial headline

## Theme Translation

For "art trash journal":

- tan newspaper base, crooked green or black painted card, glue shine, beads, torn wrappers, moss fiber, title "ART TRASH NOTE"

For "tropical":

- saturated green painted board, moss fiber, cyan beads, dried petals, raffia thread, tiny toy ladder, title "GREEN WASTE GARDEN"

For "rain":

- gray newspaper, cobalt acrylic smears, clear plastic beads like water drops, torn receipts, dark thread, title "RAIN DEBRIS"

For "coffee":

- old newspaper, espresso cardboard, stained receipt, foil wrapper, brown thread, tiny spoon-like scrap, title "CAFE WASTE"

For "spring":

- pale aged paper, green paint chip, dried petals, pearl beads, yellow torn label, title "SCRAP SPRING"

For "night":

- tan paper still visible, black painted card, bottle-green glitter or glass bits, silver foil, tiny white label, title "NIGHT JUNK"

## Prompt Skeleton

Use this structure and replace bracketed fields:

```text
Portrait [2:3 or 3:5] overhead flat-lay scan on [aged paper base], [blank-space share] visible paper, soft diffuse daylight, matte old-print texture, shallow physical shadows.

[Layout family] with [collage zone size/location], [edge shape], and one clear reading route; keep the chaos concentrated and leave [blank-space placement] quiet.

Reclaimed material anchor: [specific scraps and objects], [dominant tactile texture], [glue/tape/paint behavior], [object scale], [raised relief details].

Tiny handmade typography: title "[SHORT TITLE]", [label fragments or date marks], [typewriter/newspaper/stamp/marker voice], partial text only, no long copy, no brand names.

Color and texture: [dominant color], [accent color/material], aged paper fibers, print wear, dust, glue shine, rhinestone or bead highlights, curled paper, frayed thread, imperfect hand assembly.

Mood and constraints: [emotional temperature]; avoid clean scrapbook templates, polished stationery, retail craft kits, UI cards, glossy 3D, full-bleed fantasy scenery, cartoon cuteness, copied text, watermarks, and uncontrolled clutter.
```
