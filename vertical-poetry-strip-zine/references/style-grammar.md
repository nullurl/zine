# Style Grammar

Use this file when analyzing a reference, correcting drift, or strengthening a prompt.

## Visual DNA

- The image is almost all quiet wall or blank paper.
- The subject is a single vertical strip of handmade paper.
- The strip is a thin physical object, not a designed poster panel.
- The handwriting is the main visual event: sparse, vertical, brush-made, human, and imperfect.
- The emotional temperature is meditative and domestic, like a small poem taped to a white wall.

## Composition

- Canvas: vertical 3:4, 4:5, or 3:5.
- Wall field: 85%-95% of the frame.
- Strip width: 8%-18% of canvas width.
- Strip height: 45%-75% of canvas height.
- Default placement: centered. Slight off-center placement is allowed only when it preserves isolation.
- The strip should not touch frame edges.
- The bottom may contain a tiny signature, seal, or small handwritten note.

## Paper Strip

Use material language:

- thin rice paper
- washi strip
- warm ivory note paper
- translucent cream paper
- handmade fiber paper
- slightly uneven cut edge
- faint fold lines
- soft wrinkles
- subtle tape tab at top or bottom
- very shallow shadow on the wall

Avoid thick scroll rods, ornate mounting, framing, tassels, or decorative hanging hardware.

## Handwriting

- Direction: vertical top-to-bottom.
- Ink: vermilion, red-orange, black, charcoal, or dark brown.
- Brush behavior: uneven pressure, varied stroke thickness, small ink pools, dry-brush breaks, slight tremor, human spacing.
- Characters: large enough to form a column but not so dense that they become a paragraph.
- Signature/seal: very small, at the bottom, secondary.

Do not request a precise long phrase unless the user supplies it and it is short. Image models may distort exact characters; prioritize the visible calligraphic rhythm.

## Light And Camera

- Flat front view or very slight handheld photo angle.
- Diffuse daylight or soft gallery light.
- Low contrast, no dramatic cast shadow.
- Matte surface, no glossy paper mockup.
- Wall grain or plaster texture should be subtle.

## Color

- Default base: white, warm white, pale gray, cream.
- Red phrase: vermilion or red-orange ink as the only strong color.
- Black phrase: deep charcoal ink with tiny red seal only if needed.
- Avoid multicolor ink, bright backgrounds, or decorative color blocks.

## Drift Corrections

- Too poster-like: add `one narrow physical paper strip`, `not a graphic poster`, `huge blank wall`.
- Too dense: add `few large vertical characters`, `no paragraphs`, `tiny signature only`.
- Too wide: add `strip only 10 percent of canvas width`.
- Too decorative: add `no frame, no rods, no tassels, no collage`.
- Too clean/digital: add `hand-brushed ink, uneven pressure, dry-brush breaks, paper fibers`.
- Too dark: add `bright white wall, diffuse daylight, soft low-contrast photo`.
