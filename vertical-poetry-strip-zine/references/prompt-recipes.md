# Prompt Recipes

Use one recipe per image. Keep the wall field dominant.

## Recipe Fields

Before writing the final prompt, choose:

- Layout family
- Paper tone
- Ink color
- Phrase strategy
- Signature/seal treatment
- Tape/shadow treatment
- Camera feel

## Layout Families

### centered-strip

Use for the quietest, most reference-faithful output.

- One narrow strip centered on a white wall.
- Minimal tape visibility.
- Long vertical phrase.
- Tiny bottom signature.

Prompt phrase:
`one narrow vertical handmade paper strip centered on a vast white wall, vertical hand-brushed characters, tiny bottom signature, almost all surrounding space left empty`

### high-hung-strip

Use for a more floating composition.

- Strip sits slightly above center.
- More wall space below.
- Good for light, hope, waiting, or air themes.

Prompt phrase:
`a thin vertical poetry strip hung slightly above center with a large calm blank field below`

### low-hung-strip

Use for heavier, more grounded emotion.

- Strip sits slightly below center.
- More blank wall above.
- Good for memory, hesitation, grief, or late-night themes.

Prompt phrase:
`a narrow handwritten strip placed slightly low on a pale wall, with a vast empty upper field`

### off-center-strip

Use for quiet asymmetry.

- Strip sits slightly left or right.
- Keep all edges far from the frame.
- Do not add secondary objects.

Prompt phrase:
`one slender paper strip slightly off-center on a white wall, surrounded by asymmetrical negative space`

### taped-ribbon

Use when the paper object's physicality matters.

- Small translucent tape tabs at top and bottom.
- Faint folds and wrinkles.
- Soft wall shadow.

Prompt phrase:
`a thin washi ribbon taped to a white wall, faint tape tabs, soft wrinkles, shallow shadow, vertical brushed ink`

### double-whisper

Use only when the user asks for dialogue, contrast, or pair.

- Two neighboring strips.
- One primary strip, one fainter strip.
- Still sparse; do not fill the wall.

Prompt phrase:
`two very thin neighboring poetry strips on a quiet wall, one primary vertical phrase and one faint support line`

## Text Strategy

- Supplied phrase: use it as a short vertical phrase.
- No phrase: invent 4-10 Chinese characters that describe a feeling, not a slogan.
- Red ink: best for desire, longing, warmth, warning, or sea/return motifs.
- Black ink: best for stillness, clarity, waiting, rain, moon, or restraint.
- Tiny seal: optional with black ink; usually unnecessary with red ink unless the prompt needs a visible detail.

Example invented phrases:

- `慢慢归来`
- `风过无声`
- `月下小记`
- `心事如水`
- `山海不语`
- `一念回身`

## Prompt Skeleton

Use this structure:

```text
Vertical 4:5 sparse photographic paper poster, bright white wall or pale warm-gray wall filling about 90 percent of the frame, flat front view, diffuse daylight, no border and no mockup.

One narrow vertical [rice paper / washi / ivory handmade paper] strip [centered / slightly high / slightly low / slightly off-center], about [10-14] percent of canvas width and [55-70] percent of canvas height, faint fibers, uneven cut edge, subtle fold lines, tiny tape tabs, and a very shallow wall shadow.

Vertical hand-brushed [vermilion red / black charcoal] characters reading "[short phrase]" down the strip, uneven pressure, dry-brush breaks, human spacing, no digital font, tiny handwritten signature and small seal mark near the lower end only.

Matte photo-scan texture, soft wall grain, mild paper translucency, low contrast, quiet old zine restraint, one ink color as the only accent.

Mood: meditative, sparse, domestic gallery, handmade, poetic, memory-like. Avoid dense calligraphy scroll, framed artwork, poster layout, colorful background, table flat lay, collage, stickers, 3D mockup, glossy shadows, logo, watermark, long paragraphs.
```

## Variation Guidance

For batches, vary only one or two axes:

- red vs black ink
- centered vs off-center
- tape visible vs nearly invisible
- warmer paper vs cooler paper
- tiny seal present vs absent

Do not vary into broad calligraphy posters, framed scrolls, or room interiors.
