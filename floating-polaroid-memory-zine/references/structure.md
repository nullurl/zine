# Floating Polaroid Memory Structure

## Reverse-engineered source DNA

- One centered instant-photo-style card.
- Thick white border with a slightly matte paper texture.
- A softened, blurred background that echoes the same scene or color palette.
- The photo inside the card stays relatively crisp compared with the field outside.
- The whole composition feels like a memory object suspended in space.
- Minimal or no typography.
- Mood: dreamy, nostalgic, gentle, airy, and slightly polished.

## Layout families

- **single-center-card:** one square or near-square card centered on a blurred field.
- **single-card-offcenter:** one card slightly above or below center, leaving more breathing room on one side.
- **stacked-echo:** one main card with a very faint enlarged echo of the same image behind it.
- **soft-bloom-background:** bright floral or green scene with a blurred halo around the card.
- **misty-landscape-card:** soft sky, clouds, field, or water around a crisp central card.
- **night-glow-card:** dusk or blue-hour image with a cool blurred surround and quiet highlights.

## Card recipes

- White border, square inner image, matte paper edge, no visible plastic.
- Slight shadow under the card, but not a hard mockup shadow.
- The card may have subtle rounded corners only if the source suggests it.
- Inner crop should keep the subject recognizable and calm.

## Background recipes

- Enlarged blur of the same photo.
- Color wash matching the subject, with soft edge vignettes.
- Hazy duplicate shapes or bokeh-like diffusion only when appropriate.
- Keep the background simple and mostly abstract.

## Prompt recipe

1. Canvas and background: ratio, overall tone, blur field, quiet space.
2. Floating card: position, size, border, crop, subject focus.
3. Blur and glow: background echo, haze, soft shadow, vignette.
4. Mood and avoids: memory mood plus strict negative constraints.

## Good hard avoids

- Collage stacks
- Book spread or scrapbook layout
- Visible handwritten pages
- Dense text blocks
- Neon or glitch
- Multiple competing frames
- Busy scenery outside the card

## Example prompt skeleton

```text
Square memory poster with a large blurred background that echoes the same sky and trees as the source.

Place one centered floating instant-photo card with a thick white border and a crisp crop of the scene inside.

Let the outer field stay soft, hazy, and color-matched to the card; add a gentle shadow and slight vignette.

Keep it dreamy, minimal, and photographic; avoid collage clutter, heavy text, multiple cards, and mockup styling.
```
